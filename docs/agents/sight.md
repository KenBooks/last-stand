# What hides the hero, what he sees, what thins

From `src/core/AGENTS.md`: what stands before the camera and hides the hero, what he can see from above (the fog of what he can't), and how what's in the way thins (`heroHidden.ts`, `sight.ts`, `wholeView.ts`, `seeThrough.ts`). Paths are under `src/core/` unless they're given whole.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

**The fog of what he can't see and the thinning are drawn by the engine** (its lit shader's sight, `src/engine/sightFog.ts`, and its see kinds, `src/engine/seeKinds.ts`; `src/engine/AGENTS.md`): three's WebGL path and three's WebGPU drawing of the scene were taken out: the Rust engine alone draws the game (`src/engine/AGENTS.md`, `src/engine/STATE.md`). What he sees, the rays, what thins and by how much are live, as the game's code works them out; what's said below of the passes that read the scene, of its depth resolved, of the ghosts counted over a pixel and of a program for each renderer is how three drew them.

- `heroHidden.ts`: how far what stands before the camera hides the hero, by a few lines a frame to points of his body against the wall and the obstacles (an obstacle's `sight` where it stands taller to the eye than it stops a shot: the fountain's tiers over its 0.6 m basin never counted). While it does, all that stands free nearer than him thins alike to a ghost (`GHOST_WHILE_HIDDEN`, a uniform; `propFoot` for a thing's foot under `FLOOR_CLEAR`): a hole his own size showed him alone ("character and context around character needs to be visible even if the character is behind props"), and a wider hole cut what it met at its edge, a lantern left floating and a wall torn (two reviews). `lab village --only heroBehindProps`, a scenario each for the stair's cheek, the fountain and a lamp post. A fence (`fence`: the village's palisade, its meshes named `FENCE` in `world/wall.ts`) opens a hole of its own, by the lines it alone hides: one hole for both, the palisade hiding him at the village's edge (the camera out past it) opened the houses' 6 m wide too, and the cottage beside him went to its footing (from play, "at border of map, the house behind player suddenly disappeared"; `lab village --only housesBesideFence`, a house planted beside him at each stand). What's built (`built`: houses, the hall) and a fence have no hole over the shoulder, where the camera stands on his side of it, and the near plane comes in by a wall beside the camera (`nearClear` in `renderer.ts`: at 0.5 m it cut the wall away, showing both sides of it at once, "I should not able to see through walls like this"; `lab village --only wallsStayWhole`).
- **Only what hides him at his waist or over thins** (`WAIST` in `heroHidden.ts`, from play: "lets make exception for thinning, and dont thin if obstacle is below waist"): of the lines to his body, only those at or above 0.55 of his height count, so a parapet or the stair's cheek over his legs stands whole. What stands free thins whole or not at all (`propsHideHim`, the props' hole and their ghost held to it): a notice board half hidden thinned over its top alone, "half drawn geometry is still there"; a thing that stands free but is in the wall, which the lines meet as the wall (the board), is named `STANDS_FREE` (`world/wall.ts`) and counts as a prop. `lab village --only heroBehindProps`.
- `sight.ts`: what the hero can see from where he stands, from above alone (from play, "we have a bug that allows character to see inside buildings while staying outside"): 1024 rays round him against the walls (`SightWall`s, the village's `sight`: lines on the floor), as far as each goes kept in a strip of a texture, and each pixel he can't see fogged as the post chain starts, before the bloom (cold, nearly colourless, its light pressed into a narrow range, `SIGHT_LOOK`: solid black read as a hole in the picture, "solid black does not look good", and only dimmed, its shadows still fell to black and its white walls stayed the brightest thing there), by its place in the world read from the scene's depth (WebGL's sanitize pass, `seen`; WebGPU's `seenOnly`). Cast again only when he has moved, one program for every renderer, nothing else compiled or drawn. The scene target's depth was resolved only while it was on (WebGL's `resolveDepthBuffer`). Through the eyes and over the shoulder the camera stands where he does, and walls hide what they hide, so it's off. What stands higher than `SIGHT_LOOK.over` stays seen (a roof, a crown), and a building shut to him is much darker still, up to its walls' top. Past a door he sees through (`SightDoor`: in front of it, no further aside than its half width and `DOOR_ASIDE` of how far out he stands, within its reach, no wall between him and its middle, with optional doorway passage depth `deep`) the room is seen as from the doorway: a second set of rays, cast once a door from just inside its inner jambs (`deep + DOOR_IN`), in the strip's second row (`DOOR_ROW`), read past the door's line alone and eased in and out (`uSightDoorOn`), by every reader of the rays (the fog, `seeSeenAt`, `seenShareAt`, `inTheFog`, WebGPU's nodes). Stood against the wall beside the door, he saw the doorway edge on and the whole room came out of the fog (`--only columnsStayGhosts`). `--only doorConeOpens`, its canary the doors held off (`holdDoorsOff`). The door's rays reach only as far as his true line of sight through both the outer and inner jambs (`wedgeReach` from `(hx, hz)` directly, no forward lean past his eyes; cast again as he moves): in the doorway the widening V-cone through the 0.6 m passage, and from the stair the wedge narrowing towards his own sight (from play, "new thinning ignores line of sight": from the stair the whole room came out of the fog and the wall over the door opened end to end; `--only doorConeFromStair`, its canary `holdDoorWedgeOff`). `lab village --only sightBlacksOut`. Soft across the rays only, 9 reads of a ray each (`seenAlong`, the strip read texel by texel: filtered, two rays' bands mid-fade met as a third that neither was); along a ray, past a wall, the fog comes in within 0.5 m (`SIGHT_LOOK.wall`: softer, a green band stood outside the hall, "grass should be grey outside through a wall"). A ray's reach that jumps (it passed a corner) fades in place over `FADES_OVER`, a band of its nearer and further reach and how much of it is seen; a reach that slides follows at once; a jump within a fade carries the fade on, as a ray along a wall he's crossing the line of jumps every frame (from play, "fog of war should ease in/out, not jerk", "sometimes it's jerking left and right as I'm walking in straight line"; `lab village --only fogHoldsSteady`). A ray's fade is kept by its angle, so when the eye itself jumps (across the hall's door line, `sightEyeZ`: 0.6 m) every ray is cast as it is, at once (`EYE_JUMPS`): a reach from the other side of the line was another line's, and the portico's columns went into the fog in a frame and came back over the fade (from play, the hall's left and right columns flickered as he walked in; `--only hallEntrySteady`). Faded instead from what was shown along each new ray (found by a few rounds along it), a frame of the room stood brighter there. The roofs' map also tells within a house's walls from under its eaves (`WITHIN_WALLS`, `UNDER_EAVES`): a thinned roof lets the fog off only over ground within its walls, or the grass past a wall under the eaves stood green in the fog ("and here should be all grey, right?"; `--only fogUnderEaves`), and ground outside every roof's eaves takes no roof's ghost as a reason to let the fog off (`pastRoofs`): a far roof's overhang, its ghost lying over the grass past its walls in the frame, let it off there ("still seeing green where character cannot see"; `--only fogPastCottages`). What stands high stays seen on a roof alone, by the same map (`tSightRoofs`): by height alone a tree's lower crown read as a roof (`--only leavesInFog`). The ground seen through a thinned roof is by the rays, green where he sees it (the owner's choice: "grass that cannot be seen by me, but can be seen by player should be green"; the roof's pixel says it's one by its alpha, `tellsItsShare`): taken as unseen whatever the rays said (`uSightRoofOne`, now a harness's), the ground he saw past a house's corner lay grey under its roof, though by the rays the edge of what he sees there stands across the roof as a straight cut ("why we cut off a portion / triangle?"; `--only roofShowsWhatHeSees`). So is the ghost of a far wall through a roof, which keeps its depth: a rule that took it as unseen fogged the ground he saw past that wall through its dots (`--only roofShowsWhatHeSees` reads through a roof held thinner, `holdRoofGhost`, as at the game's too little shows to tell the rule on from off, and only where the camera's ray crosses the roof alone). Roofs stay `ROOF_GHOST_ABOVE` thinned, little enough that the ground past them doesn't take the roof's look, their hole round him widening by a share of what's built's alone (`ROOF_HOLE_WIDER`: at all of it a cottage's roof went whole, a glass box with a strip of grass across it), and the fog over a roof's eaves is by what's under them in the fog's hue and their own light (`SIGHT_LOOK.eaves`): fogged whole, the roof's colour went with it and the eaves stood round it as a plate. `inTheFog` tells a thing it needn't move: a fire there plays no frame of its flames (`world/fire.ts`, `--only firesStillInFog`).
- `wholeView.ts`: the frame drawn whole for a view that isn't the game's own, the developer mode's free camera (`src/dev/freeCam.ts` sets it, production never does; #616, from play: "in free cam mode, there is a circle beyond which grass disappears, it should not", "also in free cam we should not thin anything"). Each that reads it keeps what the game asks meanwhile and draws none of it: `updateSeeThrough` takes `props` as none (every hole's strength and every ghost's share is a share of it), `fadeWhole` and `hallSightCone` a share of none, `seeFrom` eases the game's fog on underneath (`fogAsked`) with `uSightOn` at 0, the meadow draws its rings round where the camera looks, the last out to all of it (`world/AGENTS.md`), and the game's `hallInside` leaves the meadow and the hall's room shown. Set and let go at once (`onWholeView`: a frozen game has no frame of its own to set them in), uniforms, counts and `visible` alone: no program is made, and WebGPU's nodes read the same uniforms. Something new that thins, fogs or culls for the game's camera reads `wholeView()` too. `lab village --only freeCamWhole` checks it.
- `seeThrough.ts`: all of what fades whole thinned by a share of its own (`fadeWhole`, the hall's front wall and roof over the hero inside), and the props and walls between the camera and the hero dissolved: in a cone from the eye to him, his own size where he stands, narrower across the view than up and down it (`PROP_ACROSS`: a body is a third as wide as it is tall). A tube as wide all the way, and as wide as tall, took the wall or the cottage he stood beside apart toward the camera, and the eaves over it, though they hid nothing of him (from play, "should not see through here, because I'm in the front"; `lab village` draws the frame with the hole and held shut beside a cottage and behind its roof: 20% of the frame outside him came apart, now none).
- What thins is thinned by a fine ordered pattern, a 4 by 4 Bayer matrix (`seeThrough.ts`, `shaderPorts.ts`): interleaved noise's long lines read on a roof as z-fighting ("z fighting texture at this angle"), and a hash's clumps as a broken picture (a review). A roof and what the hall thins whole blend instead (`blended` in `seeThrough.ts`, `thinBy` in `shaderPorts.ts`; from play, "Yes, real blending"): they're drawn after what's opaque, and are as solid as what's opaque to a body or a shot (`thinsByBlending`, which the wall's own test and the arrows' read: taken for glass, every wall in the village was walked through, "can walk through buildings too"). A thinned roof lets go of its depth and says how much of its pixel is what's under it in its alpha (`tellsItsShare`), which the fog alone lies over: with its depth kept the fog read the whole pixel as a roof, and the grass and walls seen through it stood bright over what he can't see, "fog of war bugs" (`lab village --only fogUnderRoofs`).
