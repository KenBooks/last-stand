# Co-op's UI

From `src/ui/AGENTS.md`: what a player sees and uses of co-op on the page (the netcode is `src/net/AGENTS.md`). Paths are under `src/ui/` unless they're given whole.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- **Co-op pings** (`ping.ts`, the pings themselves `game/ping.ts`, the message `src/net/AGENTS.md`): Left Shift held shows the ping letting go would make as a preview, faint and still, placed as the aim moves, on the ground or the foe under it (its ring and pin at `PREVIEW` strength, the foe outlined as Quarry Mark's aim does: `placePreview`), and a hint low in the middle (`.pinghint`) saying what it would ping; nothing is sent until it's let go, which pings where the preview stands, as Veilstep shows its landing while held (from play: "it needs to show ping preview on hold, and ping on release", "while I hold it should change the mark placement, ground or mob, then release to commit"). Every ping is one size, a foe's ring and pin centred on it as drawn (from play: "do not increase radius, just have same size for all... center point above mob. not side": a drag made an area and a foe's ring took its size; its pin stood by the copy's `pos`, off its body). The key was Ctrl, then G: Ctrl's keys are Chrome's too (Ctrl+W closes the tab, Ctrl+1 to 4 switch tabs, and a page can't stop them; from play, it "can change tab"). Each ping shows in the world as a ring laid on the floor (`floorPatch`, the effects' additive material: its program is warmed with them, its sample `pingSamples` in `game/warmup.ts`) with a ripple running out once every `PING.ripple` s (1.2: slower than the 1.5 a second the root allows, none with reduced motion) and a pin over it, a label of the overlay (`ORDER.ping`) naming who pinged and how far, held near the screen's edge with an arrow the way to it when it's off screen (`onScreenOrEdge` in `floaters.ts`, as a downed partner's marker is; `EDGE`, nearer the edge than the marker: in first person most pings are off screen and the arrow is the way to turn, from play "near the edge of screen is better"; its words under it at the top edge, and kept off the minimap, `offMinimap`, the minimap's box read twice a second, not every frame), in the pinger's hero colour; and on the minimap (`onMinimap` in `hud.ts`). On touch the minimap takes the finger (`body.touch #minimap`: on desktop it lets the mouse through to the game) and a finger held there (`PING.longPress`; a move past `pressSlop` before then cancels it) shows the ping, placed as it moves, made as it lifts, the browser's context menu kept off it. `npm run lab -- coop --scenarios ping` (`--pictures`: `out/coop/ping-*.png`).
- **A partner in a village house** (`houseMarks.ts`, co-op): his name over the house's door in the partners' blue (a label of the overlay, painted as it changes), a ringed dot at the door on the minimap (`onMinimap`) and his name under the house's on the village's map (`showWhoIsInside` in `villageMap.ts`, the names laid clear again as one grows), read from the room each partner's state says (`partnersInHouses` in `net/session.ts`): nothing new is sent. `npm run lab -- coop --scenarios houses`.
- **Co-op text chat** (`chat.ts`, the lines `net/chat.ts`, `styles/_chat.scss`): in a room, in the lobby and in a run (no menu, hall or saves over it), Enter opens a box to type in (Enter sends, its Send button showing an Enter key ⏎; Esc shuts it without pausing; Alt held while typing still shows the cursor over mouse look, `core/input.ts` letting Alt through the field, a click away shuts it, what was typed kept); on touch the chat button under the scores button (`#btn-chat`; the box's field at 16 px, so a phone's keyboard doesn't zoom the page). The lines show in the bottom left corner over the bars, the newest few (`CHAT.shownShut`, the stylesheet's `nth-last-child`), all of them fading together once the chat has been quiet `CHAT.idle` s (no line come, the box not opened or typed in; the open box shuts then, what was typed kept), by their opacity alone (at once with reduced motion), back with the next line (from play: "make it disappear after some time if no activity"); open, every line kept, to scroll back through. In the lobby it stands right of the hero panel (`--clear-x` from `clearOverlays` in `menus.ts`, as the overlays down the left). Each line `Name: text` on one dark see-through panel (from the owner's reference picture: one panel, not a box a line), each name in the colour its player picked, the one its hero wears (`nameColour`, lit a little to read: from play, "colour should be the colour that you chose"); a line is text, never markup. `npm run lab -- coop --scenarios chat`.
- **The watching bar** (`#spectate`, `coop.ts` `bindSpectate`, `game/spectate.ts`): out of a co-op run, at the top of the HUD, in one row, the previous partner's button, who is watched (or the free camera), the next's and Free camera / Follow, each the touch path of a key (the camera turns by two fingers twisted, as everywhere on touch: from play, "rotation of the camera should be done using regular controls on touch, why we need buttons?"); on desktop a line of the keys under them (LMB next, RMB previous, Q / E turn, F free camera; flying, WASD), hidden on touch. Its buttons are a finger's 40 css px at least and laid out alike whatever the pointer: sized for touch alone, a first tap on a screen with a mouse too put the page in touch mode as it went down and laid the bar out again under the finger, its click landing on another button. Its icons drawn (‹ and › as text were a third of the turns' size). Check with `npm run lab -- coop` (`--party 4`, `--pictures`: `out/coop/watching-bar-<n>.png`): each button tapped, the bar fitted on a desktop's, phones' and tablets' screens, within the window, over no part of the HUD (two rows on an upright tablet lay over the partners' frames) and no further than 40% down, over the hero it follows.
- **The players' name tags** (`updateNameTags` in `coop.ts`, every frame in a room): over every hero in the room, our own too, in the lobby and in a run, the name over its life bar; none over a hero away in the other place, hidden (the hall of heroes hides the partners: `game/hall.ts` every frame, as a partner joining or reset was shown again among its heroes, and a hidden partner plays no casts, `Player.replay`), fallen, or our own through our eyes. From play: "name tags should be visible above other players in lobby, and above me as well". `npm run lab -- coop` holds every hero each game shows to its tag drawn, every frame of the scenarios and the lobby after them, and opens the host's hall of heroes as the others cast in their lobbies.

  occupied colours are disabled and a conflicting in-flight request keeps the existing colour. Settings refreshes

  40 actual css pixels after UI zoom. The Settings palette spans both columns; a short touch screen's co-op dialog

  (`order`): their row pushed Open a room under the fold of a phone held sideways asked to rename. Check with

  hero's cloth drawn in it).
- **The Play together dialog asks for the player's name tag** (`coop.ts`, `net/nameTag.ts`): a field over Host and Join, filled with the tag kept from last time, checked as Open a room or Join is pressed (said under it and focused if it can't be used); Enter in it joins when a code is typed. Refused for a tag the room already has, the dialog says so, marks the field and selects its tag, the room's code kept, so the player retypes and joins again. On touch it is a plain text field: a tap focuses it and the phone's keyboard comes up (`enterkeyhint="done"`). On a phone held sideways it fits whole, Close too, without scrolling: a modal box's limits are the window's over `--ui` (`_modals.scss`), as it's zoomed by `--ui`, and Chrome zooms its own `vh` with it (a phone's 86vh, zoomed by 0.6, held the dialog to half the screen and scrolled it; zoomed over 1 it reached past the window and lost its top, "panel cut off"). Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states coop,coopRename,coopTouch` at each screen `src/ui/AGENTS.md` lists and a phone's browser tab (`832x320@2.8125`) (`page/uiCoop.ts`: the dialog within the window and not scrolling, each of its controls found at its middle, the rename's tag marked, named and focused with the code kept, a finger's tap focusing the field; `--canary` moves Open a room off the window's foot).
- **What's typed in Play together stays through its renders** (`typed`, `fieldTypedIn` in `coop.ts`, #555): a colour or portrait picked, the server's check answered and every change of the session render the dialog again, and each rebuilt the name tag from the one kept, so a name typed was gone by the time a code was. The field typed in keeps the focus and its caret; a fresh refusal or invite shows its own tag and code (`formFor`). Open a room or Join pressed while the heroes are cold (`whenWarm`) says so on the button pressed ("Opening a room…", "Joining…") and over the choices, both disabled with the fields until the room is asked for (`pressed`); then the room's view says what it does ("Joining room X…"; hosting, "Opening the room on the co-op server…" till its link is open, `session.opening`). Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states coop,coopRename,coopTouch` at each screen `src/ui/AGENTS.md` lists: the coop state types a tag and pastes a code, picks a colour and renders the dialog with the code focused, then presses Open a room and Join with the heroes cold (`chill`); the rename state retypes the taken tag and renders it again.
- Optional party portraits (#396): `portraitPicker.ts`, `portraitArt.ts`, `portraits/`. The closed picker makes no unchosen image requests; opening loads only the current class’s enabled male choices. Selected pictures appear only in lobby/fight party lists, never name tags or chat. None is the default, and shows the class's default picture in its place (`classOutlines.ts`, `portraits/<class>/default-<class>.webp`: a bust in outline, a pale line over a grey fill, no face, inlined in the bundle so it's no request; a plain SVG bust for a class with none painted), so every card and list row is laid out alike whoever chose a portrait (from the owner: "this way all cards will always have the same format"; drawn as flat SVG shapes first: "we need them fancy, like proper triple A quality"). A new class gets one drawn (`portraits/AGENTS.md`). HUD images refresh only when the ID changes. Each class’s preference is independent of save slots.
- The local player's co-op card is prepended above partners, in addition to appearing first in the room list. It has a plain 3px golden border on the left only and no You prefix. Party cards put the player name left and the class right on one line, in the class's Hero Selection accent. Only the host (slot 0) has a small gold star over the bottom-left of their portrait, with an accessible Host label; no extra text row changes the card layout. Portraits, health and status keep their existing update path. Leaving co-op clears all cards, including the self card; short touch screens use compact cards so four players fit.
- Portraits use five color/outfit variants per class (IDs 11–15), in one five-column row. None is a separate control above it. The Ranger's five portraits are cropped from the owner's cinematic sheet (blue/red hoods, green headband, purple cloak, ochre cloak with tied hair); the source and crop coordinates are recorded in `portraits/prompts.json`. Original artwork remains preserved and unbundled.

- **The trade window** (`trade.ts`, `#trade` in `index.html`, `styles/_trade.scss`; the trade itself `net/trade.ts`): asked from a partner's Trade button in Play together's list (`tradeButton` in `coop.ts`, off with why while it can't be asked); the ask and its answer (Trade or Decline; Cancel while asking) and the last word of a trade (done, or off and why) stand at the top's middle under the room's bar (`#trade-ask`, `#trade-note`). The window: each side's offer (12 at most, `TRADE.most`) side by side, both accepts under them, our stash under them (scrolling inside; what the partner's class can't use dimmed, saying why), the line saying where it stands or why Accept can't be pressed, Accept and Cancel; Esc cancels (`handleGlobalKeys`), and it's a dialog for the pointer and the village's input (`dialogOpen`). Every item has its tooltip; with a mouse a click picks it, and on touch a tap pins its tooltip with a button in its foot that picks it (a tap acting at once left the tooltip pinned over Accept, and a first tap pinning and a second acting moved nothing where the tooltip, laid out to fit, covered the item). On a short screen (height ≤ 520) the stash stands beside the offers, the wider of the two (at an even share the offers' squares ran past a 751x298 phone's window). `lab coop --scenarios trade` fits it on the six screens of `TRADE_SCREENS` by mouse and by touch (`--pictures`: `out/coop/trade-fit-*.png`).
