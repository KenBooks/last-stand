# The village's houses walked into

From `src/world/AGENTS.md`, by way of `village.md`: the houses' rooms (`rooms.ts`, the kit; `houses.ts`, which finds them; `houses/<id>.ts`, each one's build; `merchantWares.ts`), shown in the village's stead. The rest of the village is `village.md`. Paths are under `src/world/` unless they're given whole.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- **Its houses walked into** (`rooms.ts`, which and what stands in each in `data/houses.ts`, taken in and out by `game/rooms.ts`): the house of records (the cottage nearest the notice board), the tavern, The Ember & Oak (the cottage east of the square, its door on the road to the Crypt), and the library (`library.ts`: the cottage south of the square between the campfire and the training yard; its Codex opens the in-game guide). Each is a small room built at boot beside the places (`buildRooms`, only with the village) and shown in the village's stead (`Arena.showRoom`), as the hall of heroes' room is: a house seen open from the village would add its inside to the heaviest place to draw. While one is shown, `Arena.wall` and `obstacles` are the room's (the boom stops at its walls, `keepIn` holds him in: its shell alone is in its `Wall`, its furniture circles, as rays from the middle meet a table before a wall), its floor a flat relief (`dais: false`), and the village's lamps are out (`lightLamps`' `shown`, `castFromLamps(false)` on `roomShown`). **Its still parts are drawn as one a material** (`mergeRoom` in `rooms.ts`, after its static shadows are baked, as the village's are by `mergeStill`): built a mesh a part, the merchant's shop drew 853 times from above. Kept apart (`keepApart`): what moves (the jars, the duster and the dagger Odo handles, `thing` in `houses/merchant.ts`), each seat (outlined as the cursor points at it and sat on by its top), the scholar's desk (read by name, `lab scholar`, and he sits in it); merged within themselves and kept apart, what's shown and hidden as one (each cut away wall and the ceiling, each trophy) and the chandeliers (`lab houses` finds them by name). A new part that moves, is shown and hidden or is read by name is kept apart where it's built; a merge is made in the room's own frame (`mergeUnder` in `props.ts`: a wall's group is turned to its side); a mesh with parts of its own stays out of it (`mergeable`: merged and taken from the scene, the library's open books lost their pages with their blocks); and a mirrored part keeps its faces' winding (`placedPart`: the tavern's axe head on its shield, `scale.x` -1, turned inside out and was culled). `lab houses --ab` pictures each room against main's. **As a biome, 10 point lights, two casting** (the hearth and the chandelier): a candle or a lantern with a light is one of the ten, the rest are flames alone (`candle` without a light). **No light under a cut away wall**: the walls and the ceiling the camera stands outside of are hidden (`cutAway`, a wall's inner face plus `OUTSIDE`: the boom stops short of it, so over the shoulder and through the eyes none is), and a light hidden with them would change the lights' count and compile every lit program; a lantern by the door hangs on a side wall. The house of records' boards, ledger pages and plaques are canvases written as he comes in (`showRecords`: a texture's upload, no program), its trophies (`TROPHY_LOOKS`, one look per `TrophyDef.look`, `crest` needing nothing of a new boss) shown by visibility, all of them drawn in its warm-up (`showAll`). **Its doors** (`door.ts`, from play: "a proper detailed door"): a timber frame, a leaf of planks on ledges strapped by three iron hinges, a ring and a lock plate, each part merged into a mesh a material, in materials the place has (a cottage's leaf and iron are clones of its planks, `doorLeaf`, `doorIron`: one program), shut and still (swung open as E was held, the leaf stood out at an angle before a dark hole and read as broken: from play, "Don't have the door animation, remove it"). In the village a house's cottage has one in place of its plain slab, its frame standing 0.2 m off the wall so the leaf reads as set into it and two stone steps up to its sill from the ground (`DOOR_STEPS`: the slab stood on the plinth with no way up, from play), merged with what stands still, and the house's name painted on a sign over it (`doorSign`), as the signposts' arms are (`signPaint.ts`: ivory, heavy dark capitals, held under the bloom; from Viktor, of its dark board and gilt letters: "labels on houses should be high contrast as well"). A door's frame stands a hair off the wall's face and its hole is lined, the lining a millimetre off the hole's faces: its jambs stood half into the wall's thickness, their faces in the plaster's beside the hole and under its head, which flickered seen from inside (from play). In the room the side walls stop at the front's and back's faces, and what runs along them (the wainscot, its rail, the skirting, the plate) stops short by its own depth, the posts under the plate: run into the corners, their tops lay in one plane and flickered from above. `npm run lab -- houses` (`--canary`) holds the door and reads the faces round it. **The library's books** are one instanced mesh a spine design (`SPINES`, each spine a canvas: bands, ribs, a label; the book's colour its instance's, so a spine's gold takes its leather's tint), every book of every case in them, a few draws for the whole room; its cases are circles along their fronts (`bookcase`'s), the wall behind them the room's. **The library's scholar**, Brother Hollis, reads at his table (`scholarDesk`, where in `SCHOLAR_DESK`; the reading desk he took the place of gave him its candle's light, so the room keeps its ten). He's seated after the rooms are built (`Room.seatFolk`, from main.ts once the heroes' heads and hands are made by their workers: his head and hands are theirs, and built with the room he'd have sculpted them on the page), so he is in neither the room's baked static shadow (`bakeStaticShadows` would have frozen his moving parts' shadow) nor WebGPU's still meshes (`stillForMeshlets`); the rooms' warm-up draws him. He's posed by the room's updater, only while the library is shown. What stands on his table keeps clear of where his arms go (his forearms lie on it from its near edge to the book, his left hand slides onto it beside the book as he turns a page): his quill stood in its inkwell there and poked up through his sleeve (`lab scholar` reads his table's things, named `scholar desk ...`, against his sleeves). Talked to through `scholarTalk.talking` (main.ts sets it as the talk card opens and closes). `npm run lab -- scholar`. A module the rooms' builder imports (`library.ts`) reads nothing of `rooms.ts` as it loads: they import each other, and its look made at load threw (`ROOM_LOOK` before it was made) and stopped the boot.
- **A house's chandelier hangs clear of its beams** (`chandelier` in `rooms.ts`, its parts in a group named `chandelier`, the ceiling's named `ceiling`; from play, "Fix light fixture, it goes through a beam"): the ridge beam's underside is `RIDGE_UNDER` (0.5 m) under the ceiling and the cross beams' `CROSS_UNDER` (0.26), one every 1.6 m; a chandelier under one hangs `CHANDELIER_DROP` lower than the ceiling, or centred under a cross beam, its chains meeting at that beam's underside (`hook`), its candles' flames 0.14 to 0.18 m under it. `npm run lab -- houses` holds every part of each room's chandeliers clear of the ceiling's meshes, box against box (`fixturesClear`; `--canary` lifts one into its beam).
