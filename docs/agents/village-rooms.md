- **Its houses walked into** (`rooms.ts`, which and what stands in each in `data/houses.ts`, taken in and out by `game/rooms.ts`): the house of records (the cottage nearest the notice board), the tavern, The Ember & Oak (the cottage east of the square, its door on the road to the Crypt), and the library (`library.ts`: the cottage south of the square between the campfire and the training yard; its Codex opens the in-game guide). Each is a small room built at boot beside the places (`buildRooms`) and shown in the village's stead (`Arena.showRoom`), as the hall of heroes' room is: a house seen open from the village would add its inside to the heaviest place to draw. While one is shown, `Arena.wall` and `obstacles` are the room's (the boom stops at its walls, `keepIn` holds him in: its shell alone is in its `Wall`, its furniture circles, as rays from the middle meet a table before a wall), its floor a flat relief (`dais: false`), and the village's lamps are out (`lightLamps`' `shown`, `castFromLamps(false)` on `roomShown`). **Its still parts are drawn as one a material** (`mergeRoom` in `rooms.ts`, after its static shadows are baked, as the village's are by `mergeStill`): built a mesh a part, the merchant's shop drew 853 times from above. Kept apart (`keepApart`): what moves (the jars, the duster and the dagger Odo handles, `thing` in `houses/merchant.ts`), each seat (outlined as the cursor points at it and sat on by its top), the scholar's desk (read by name, `lab scholar`, and he sits in it); merged within themselves and kept apart, what's shown and hidden as one (each cut away wall and the ceiling, each trophy) and the chandeliers (`lab houses` finds them by name). A new part that moves, is shown and hidden or is read by name is kept apart where it's built; a merge is made in the room's own frame (`mergeUnder` in `props.ts`: a wall's group is turned to its side); a part merged within itself takes its own meshes alone, never those of another such part within it (`withinOf`: the trophies hang on a wall's group, merged within itself, and merged into it each was shown with the wall, its boss beaten or not, on main as well as here); a mesh with parts of its own stays out of it (`mergeable`: merged and taken from the scene, the library's open books lost their pages with their blocks); and a mirrored part keeps its faces' winding (`placedPart`: the tavern's axe head on its shield, `scale.x` -1, turned inside out and was culled). `lab houses --ab` pictures each room against main's. **As a biome, 10 point lights, two casting** (the hearth and the chandelier): a candle or a lantern with a light is one of the ten, the rest are flames alone (`candle` without a light). **No light under a cut away wall**: the walls and the ceiling the camera stands outside of are hidden (`cutAway`, a wall's inner face plus `OUTSIDE`: the boom stops short of it, so over the shoulder and through the eyes none is), and a light hidden with them would change the lights' count and compile every lit program; a lantern by the door hangs on a side wall.
