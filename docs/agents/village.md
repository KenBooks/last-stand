# The village

From `src/world/AGENTS.md`: the lobby's village, its houses, hall, lamps, shadows, paving, fountain and ways out. Its objects at work are `src/game/AGENTS.md`'s. Paths are under `src/world/` unless they're given whole.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

**How the village is drawn is the engine's now**: three's WebGL path and three's WebGPU drawing of the scene were taken out: the Rust engine alone draws the game (`src/engine/AGENTS.md`, `src/engine/STATE.md`). The plan, the places, what's built still and merged, the sight and what thins, and what a level cuts are live. What's said below of three's lit programs and their light counts, the pooled lights casting at the lamps, the kept shadow maps drawn over, the lamps' atlas and three's passes is how three drew it, kept for what was measured; the engine has its own lamps, shadows and light cache (`src/engine/AGENTS.md`), and a `lab village` check that reads three's maps or counts may read nothing.
