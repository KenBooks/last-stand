# Loading and choosing a hero

From `src/ui/AGENTS.md`: the loading screen, the hero selection screen, the fight's loading bar and what is laid out under the loading screen. Paths are under `src/ui/` unless they're given whole.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- **A pick on the hero selection screen eats the click after it** (`heroSelect.ts` `eatClickAfter`): a tap or click on the hero closes the screen as the press lifts, and a finger's tap is followed by the browser's click at that point, which landed on the lobby under it: on a 640x360 phone the hero stands over Enter the Arena, so a first visit's pick of the mage pressed Start, which waited for the cold fight under its loading bar and then began the run ("it entered the area, enemies spawned and started attacking me, happens every time"). The next click within `CLICK_AFTER_MS` is kept from the page. Check with `npm run lab -- heropick --screen 640x360@3` (and a phone's `832x384@2.8125`, a desktop's, `--renderer webgpu`; `--canary` lets the click through).
- **The hero selection screen's Look page** (`heroLook.ts`, `.hs-look` in `styles/_heroes.scss`, the panel's second tab): the hero in focus's looks (a button each, his name for it and what sets him apart: two a row, four on an upright screen) and the hair colours (a swatch each, his look's own first, ringed dashed; 40 css px on touch), its note naming the colour picked. A pick is kept in his profile and shown on him at once, another look behind its button's "Readying…" while he's built again (`restyleHallHero`); the lobby's hero takes it as the screen closes. Buttons alone, so a tap is a click on touch. Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states heroes` at every screen above.
- **The hero selection screen's heroes warm after the lobby shows** (`heroSelect.ts` `readied`, `game/hall.ts`): a hero not yet warm stands off his plinth, his tab has a bar filling as he warms, and the choice on him says how far along he is ("Readying the Mage 40%") and chooses nothing till he's warm. Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states heroesWarming`.
- **The fight's loading bar** (`readying.ts`, `#readying` in `index.html`, `styles/_readying.scss`): the lobby shows before the fight's and the other heroes' programs are warm (`game/warm.ts`). Pressed early, Start, Change hero, a save slot, biome or co-op pick dims the lobby, shows the loading screen's bar in a small panel with the warm-up's progress, and goes ahead once it's done (on a first visit 7 to 8 s on the owner's machine, its fight compiled with nothing in Chrome's cache; a return's fight is warmed behind the lobby once the loading screen has left, about 3.5 s after the lobby shows). It's rastered under the loading screen, shown full and hidden again as the lobby is entered (`layOutReadyingAhead`), as the HUD is: first shown by a first visit's Start, Chrome built its programs (its shadows, its gradient, its text) on the GPU's process with ANGLE's threads busy compiling the fight's, and one waited 121 ms for a thread, the bar's first frames held 132 ms (`npm run stalls -- --scenario boot --angle`: `MainLinkLoadEvent::wait`). It says what the latest press waiting readies (`READYING`, each press's `whenWarm` label: "Readying the heroes", "Readying slot 2"): the page's own "Readying the fight" showed for a class pick too. Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states readyingHeroes` (Change hero clicked with the heroes kept cold, and every label on one line on the screen). A co-op guest's start shows it too, "Joining the fight", warm or not, from the host's start till its run has begun, over the hall of heroes, the save slots and the dialogs (`#readying.join`, z-index 45; `showJoining`, `src/net/AGENTS.md`), and the host's party frame of a guest still readying says how far it is (`Readying 40%`, `.pf-state`); `npm run lab -- coop` checks both.
- **The hero selection screen** (`heroSelect.ts`, `#heroes` in `index.html`, `styles/_heroes.scss`): every class's hero in 3D in the hall of heroes (`game/hall.ts`, its room `world/heroHall.ts`, behind the door of the hall on the village's terrace), the camera on the one in focus at once as it opens and as it's left (it flew in: "just show heroes immediately"), gliding along the row to a hero picked (cut there too for a while, from play "we lost camera animation when selecting characters"; `lab village --only hallShownAtOnce`, whose canary cuts), the hero left standing at rest at once (his showcase played out, he stood on one bent knee: `--only heroLeftStands`) and the one in focus showing off a skill of his (`HallDef.showcase`), his card in a panel (role, tagline, traits, skills, his records in the save slot) and the choice ("Play the X", or "Stay the X" for the lobby's own) on him: a label, not a button (from play: "remove button that appears... just make it a label with not separate hover effects"), the pointer going through it to the backdrop, where a click on the hero chooses him and a drag pans the scene; kept on him every frame as the camera shows him (`placeChoice`, from `main.ts`, moved by its transform alone on a layer of its own: placed once where the camera would come to rest, it hung there as the camera glided or was dragged, "it currently moves when I scroll"), shown as the mouse comes over him (his outline), on touch always; in the colour of the hero it stands on (his class's accent, as his name below: from play, "match color of label on hero on hover to the hero color we are using below"); over another hero it stands on him and says "Select the X", as a click there brings him into focus (`labelled`; from play: "missing label for non current hero") (in the panel's corner, the owner wanted it "on the hero... on hover"). The hover takes a box round him and what he holds, above his head too (`HOVER_SIDE`, `HOVER_ABOVE`: a box his body's width missed the gap between the staff and the mage). A first visit opens on it (`hasSavedClass` in `loot/profile.ts`: no class cookie and no profile in the slot), the loading screen's logo blown away, as it has no logo of its own to gather into ("we dont need last stand logo in hero picker"; `loading.ts` `blowFor`); the hall's dais opens it again, or the panels' Change hero (through `whenWarm(['heroes'])`), and Back or Esc closes it but on a first visit. Arrows, the heroes' names, a tap or click on a hero, the scene dragged and the keys (←/→ or A/D, Enter or Space to choose) move the focus. The hero under the mouse is outlined in white (`lightHero`, `heroAt`: the outline Quarry Mark draws, `overlay/outline.ts`; its masks are warmed with each hero, `game/warmup.ts`), and a click or tap on the hero in focus chooses him, as his choice does (from play: "should be able to click whole hero... hover outline shows then click to choose"). The number keys pick the heroes in the row's order (1, 2, 3, the number row's or the numpad's), each hero's number in the rhombus under his name (made bigger to hold it, from play: "numbers need to be in existing rhombus below label, not new squares"; on touch the rhombus is bare). The name or number of the hero already in focus, clicked, tapped or pressed again, chooses him as his choice does (`pick`; from play: "pressing these titles (only if selected title), would also play / start the game", "123 to select each, ... same number again to play"). A drag pans the camera along the row with the finger or the mouse (a hero's spacing on the screen a hero along, `dragHall`), and let go it lands on the hero nearest, carried on by a flick (`FLICK_MS`); the backdrop takes its own touches (`touch-action: none`: the browser's pan took them, and the page never heard the move). The cards' parts lie on rows they share (a subgrid), so nothing in the panel moves as the hero changes: in a column each, the ranger's tagline on two lines put his traits and skills 30 px lower. A card comes in by a fade alone: it slid up 10 px as it came, which read as the panel shifting "like its adjusting font". The heroes' names are held to the window's width too, clear of Back. The panel has the lobby's right pane's tabs too ("pro users will probably keep at inventory"): the hero's card, his Equipment (his gear round his class's doll and his stash, `heroGear.ts`) and his Attributes (`renderAttributes` given the hall's copy and his profile), the one last chosen kept (`last-stand.hero-page` in local storage) and its pages in one cell, so the panel's size never follows a page. The gear is to look at, not to change ("equipment tab is locked in hero selection screen, but hover on items should still work"): he isn't the lobby's hero until he's chosen, so his items show their cards on hover (a tap on touch) and nothing clicks, drags or throws. The save slot under the title opens the save slots over the screen (Esc closes them first, `main.ts` gives them the keys before it), and a slot played there opens the hall again at once on its heroes (`heroSelectSlotSwitched`, from `switchSaveSlot`). The panel stands at the right, or along the foot of an upright screen; the hero is framed in the room it leaves (`layOut`: the view shifted by `heroSelectShift`, the camera further back and looking at his middle on an upright screen, `frameHall`). Check at every screen `src/ui/AGENTS.md` lists with `npm run lab -- ui --states heroes` (the hero framed head to feet in the room the panel leaves, the parts none over another, the choice's words within it: the buttons' own 24 px ran past it on a phone; the choice on the hero, shown on hover; the panel's parts in place for every hero and still as each card comes in; the tabs' pages, the gear read-only with its cards; the save slot clicked and slot 2 played from it; a finger's drag followed and landed where it carried the row).
- **Loading screen:** `#loading` (markup, SVG logo and styles) is inline in `index.html` so it paints before the bundle. The game renders behind it but only advances once it fades (`revealed` in `main.ts`), so the lobby intro is seen. Tips are in `data/tips.ts`. The lobby's panels are laid out under it from the arena's end (`layOutMenuAhead` in `menus.ts`, their content rendered with the hero): their first raster builds Skia's programs on the GPU process's main thread one after another (a first visit's 86, 1.2 s), and painted with the hero the lobby's warm-up waited behind them; now they're built while he is (`npm run stalls -- --scenario boot --links` counts each step's by where they were built). The run's HUD and its banner are shown under it too (`layOutHudAhead` in `hud.ts`, hidden as the lobby is entered): first rastered after the fight's loading bar, a first visit's run held a frame up 285 ms for their 26 programs. `banner` begins its fade again by its animations, never by a size read (which laid the page out in the middle of a run's start, 11.5 ms). Anything the boot reads back from the GPU's process (a canvas's pixels, a program's locations) waits behind that raster too: read it early, or on the CPU (`readCtx2d`, `core/AGENTS.md`); the logo's program is read as soon as it links. Its exit (`ui/logoWind.ts`) snapshots the SVG letters (the font is inlined, since an SVG image can't load web fonts) onto the device pixel grid, turns every pixel into a WebGL point, blows them away and gathers them into the lobby logo (`#menu .logo-art`, kept hidden until then). The motion is closed-form in the vertex shader. Each point is kept as its place in the letters, and the letters, the lobby logo and the canvas are measured again on every frame of the exit (the lobby logo snapshotted again once it has moved or changed size, its old snapshot read where it is till then): made as the lobby was laid out and placed then, a window resized on the loading screen (full screen and back) blew the letters from where they had been and gathered them where the lobby's logo had been, 106 px and 22 px off. Check with `npm run stalls -- --scenario boot --resize 1920x1080` (five trips, the first held till the exit is made ready; `--stale-wind` is its canary). No copy of a logo is ever shown at rest beside the other, so the two never visibly swap: a point is drawn only once the wind lifts it (the real letters are clipped away just behind the front), and landing points take the colours of a snapshot of the lobby logo and land into it: the field draws the snapshot under them on the canvas's own pixels, each column shown as its points land (evenly over `LAND_SPREAD`), a point gone as it lands, so their last picture is the snapshot exactly. The lobby logo is then shown as that snapshot (`showPicture`: a canvas on the same pixels, in its letters' place) in the frame the field goes, till the logo moves or changes size or the screen its pixel ratio, when its SVG shows again. (The logo uncovered under landed points that faded jumped as it took over, a pixel down and a quarter aside at 1536x864: the browser paints the SVG a little otherwise than its snapshot, the grain and the edges, and points landing on one pixel added up to a darker rim and shadow. Its places were all exact.) Check with `npm run lab -- wind` (`--resize 1920x1080@1.25,1536x864@1.25 --screen 1536x864@1.25`: full screen and back at 125%): the particles' picture as each column rests against the logo after, at the screen's pixels; and look at it with `npm run stalls -- --scenario boot --film-logo`. It scales to the device: touch and low-end machines get a ~120k point budget (a coarser snapshot and a 2× canvas) instead of 1.5M. A watchdog cuts it short when the median of its first 10 frames is over 50 ms (under ~20 fps), and counts the launches in a row it did (`last-stand.logo-wind-slow`; one that keeps up clears the count). It falls back to a plain fade with reduced motion, Low graphics quality, or after two launches in a row cut short. Judged by the frames' mean and switched off for good on one launch (`last-stand.logo-wind-off`, cleared now), a single stall in those frames (a first visit's compiles, the GPU busy elsewhere) turned the blow off on a 4070 Ti: check with `npm run stalls -- --scenario boot --stall-blow 600` (the page held up once in the blow's first frames: the logo blown away on both visits, where production's is cut short and then a plain fade). The plain fade ends on `#loading`'s own `transitionend` (`loadingDone`): its parts' transitions bubble up to it, and the first to end (the bar's fill, the tip's fade) cut its 0.9 s off at 0.15 to 0.6 s, the loading screen gone in a frame. Nothing over the page shows or redraws under it: the overlays down the left (`#diag`, `booting` till `loadingLeft`) stay off until it's gone, as on the owner's phone the performance overlay's redraws there (4 a second) flickered the loading screen as it left; closed, it didn't. Film the exit with `npm run stalls -- --scenario boot --film` (a phone's: `--perf --window 799x384 --scale 3.75 --auto low@518400`): each frame, the flickers found, and when the loading screen was gone. **Its layers must fit a phone's memory for tiles** (Android's Chrome has at most 256 MB for a page's, the screen's pixels at 3.75 a css px on the owner's S25 Edge): past it, tiles are dropped and drawn again, and the whole loading screen blinked black, or let the hall show through, for a frame at a time on a first visit (from play: "the screen flicker a lot before the hero selection screen showed"; "whole screen blinked"). Its smoke was two SVG turbulence filters at 150% of the window (one scaled 1.3 more), masked, rastered at the screen's pixels; it's drawn once into two 600x400 canvases now, its mask in them (index.html's `ldSmoke`, which the lab's `wind` calls on the copy it puts back), which the compositor draws at their own pixels. Check with `npm run stalls -- --scenario boot --film-boot --touch --window 799x384 --scale 3.75 --gpu-memory 64` (the whole boot filmed as a phone's with a quarter of its budget: main blacked the loading screen out at 64 and 128 MB on a first visit, this at neither; at 32 every build blinks, the film's canary); `--variants noSmoke` takes the smoke out to see its share. Every boot also lists what the page changed under the loading screen while it covered it (`under the loading screen`), an element changed in more than four moments named `REDRAWN`: the performance overlay's redraws there flickered it before.
