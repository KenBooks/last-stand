# The village's folk and the scarecrow

From `src/entities/models/AGENTS.md`: the figures built outside `MODELS` (the library's scholar, Odo the merchant, the Strawman's scarecrow). Paths are under `src/entities/models/` unless they're given whole.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- **The library's scholar** (`scholar.ts`, seated by `world/houses/library.ts`; not a `Model`, so not in `MODELS`): the heroes' rig sat on a chair, the mage's head (`HERO_HEADS.mage`, so the workers' geometry: its locks hidden under a felt cap, the shell under them and the crop greyed white) and the heroes' hands, bare; a wool habit of rigid pieces on the spine, chest, hips and arms (`rowsSurface`: rows bottom to top, each running the way the right hand's fingers curl round them, wind out), a card beard, moustache and brows on the head (`hairCard`, exported from `head.ts`) in a white copy of the head's card material (its kit hooks and `bothSidesOut` carried over by hand: `clone` drops them). His beard is bent forward below the chin, as it hangs while he reads with his head bowed; rigid on the head, hung straight it swung into his chest. His loop (`SCHOLAR_LOOP`) is functions of its time alone: each beat a pulse eased in and out, every sine a whole number of turns of the loop, the page he reads switched at a turn's middle while the turn hides it, so it joins its start with no snap. **His arms lean on the table** as a reader's do: each elbow's pole aims at a rest on it a hand in from its edge (`restL`, `restR`), each forearm laid along it to the book, and his elbow stays on it as a hand goes to his face. His sleeves are shaped from that rest (`dressBells`, after his first pose: the way down from each arm in its own frame): a wide bell drops that way and lies flat 3 cm under the arm at the wrist, the upper sleeve 4.4 cm under it at the elbow, spread out at the sides as cloth on a table is. Hung free with the elbows short of the table's edge below its top, the bells went 10 cm through the table and the forearms rose through its edge (from play); `lab scholar` reads every point of his sleeves against it each frame. His outfit: the habit, a tabard of oxblood wool (`tabardMap`: a gold border, the library's sign of a book under a flame on the chest, a key on the lap) from the cowl to his knees in two strips (the chest's on the chest, the lap's laid on the skirt's top), gold cords at the cuffs and hem, keys on a ring and a leather pouch at the belt. **His book is one book** (`book.ts`, shared by `world/houses/library.ts`'s `openBook`, the page he turns and `lab scholar`): each half a block of pages from its outer edge to the spine, its top curving up from the gutter to a crest and down to its edge (`pageHeight`), the printed page over it edge to spine. Two boxes, each with a print laid on it, left a plain strip and a dark gap at the spine, and the page lifted out of that strip (from play: "fill in the pages... they should reach the middle"). **The page he turns is the right half's top page**, its front that half's print and its back the left half's, each laid on as it lies on its half, so nothing changes as it shows and hides; each of its points turns round the spine's foot from its place on the right to its place mirrored on the left (`turnedPage`), so it never passes through either half, and it shows until it lies flat on the left page. He pinches it by its outer edge, his hand beyond the edge (the falling page sweeps only within its width of the spine), his fingers on top as he takes it and under it by the time he lets go, a third of the way over (`LET_GO`), so it falls away from them, and his hand stays there till it's past upright (following it over, his hand turned 7.8° a frame letting go; with his hand over the page, the page ran into his fingers, and going back at once it went under the page still raised, from play: "it goes right through his hand"). His left hand slides off the book onto the table beside it before the page comes over, nearer him so his elbow stays bent and up on the table (reached straight out, it dropped below the table's top), and back onto the new page once it lies there. His forearms run in his undershirt's linen sleeve from inside each bell to the wrist (`undersleeve`; an empty bell with a hand at its end looked cut off: "the wrists are floating"). His hands rest on the pages, each wrist on its page's near half, the fingers nearly flat and lifted 6°, so a fingertip's pad (about a centimetre) lies on the print. Never switch an IK pole or target at a threshold: blend it with the beat's weight (a pole switched as a beat began turned his shoulder 6.8° in a frame). `npm run lab -- scholar` (the loop posed through, its glance and his eye while talked to, pictures).
- **The village's folk:** `merchant.ts`, Odo the merchant behind his counter (`world/houses/merchant.ts` seats him through `Room.seatFolk`, once the heads' and hands' workers are done, `main.ts`): the heroes' rig, head (the ranger's face, his hair and beard darkened) and sculpted hands, built as Brother Hollis is (#687) but drawn to read nothing like him (from review: "why Odo looks like librarian?": both had a grey beard, spectacles, a felt cap and an oxblood front over brown): stocky, a saffron shirt with its sleeves rolled over bare forearms, a teal waistcoat, a wide belt with pouches, a purse and keys, a short tan apron, a broad teal hat with a yellow plume; nothing past his hips but the apron (a coat's skirt or a bib read as a habit). Put them side by side in one light before changing either's look. Not a `Model`: an `Odo` with its own `update`, a loop of `ODO_LOOP` s that never snaps (breathing; weighing a coin on the scale; writing in the ledger; a glance at the hero as he nears, `GLANCE`) and a nod when a trade is made (`odoNods`). What he holds is scaled to 0.001 when not in use, never hidden, so it's warmed with the room. Look at him with `npm run lab -- merchant` (`odo-close`, `odo-counter`).
- **The Strawman's scarecrow** (`scarecrow.ts`, raised by `combat/skills/strawman.ts`; not a `Model`, so not in `MODELS`): made as its skill's icon draws it, a rough post and cross arm braced by two stakes, an olive coat patched, buttoned and belted with rope, straw tied off at the cuffs and spilling from the neck and the hem, a stuffed sack head with a stitched face (a button for one eye) and straw hair, under a frayed straw hat with a red band, worn nearly level (tipped well back to show the face, it read as fallen over: "it would not be leaned down so much"). The hat is what the top view reads it by: the first one, olive under a dark felt hat, was lost against the forest's floor. Its geometry and three materials are made once (`build`, seeded) and shared by every cast, never disposed: a cast is five meshes on them (7.4k triangles: the body's wood, cloth and straw, and the head's cloth and straw on a joint of its own, which lolls after a blow), three of them casting. The cloth and the wood are the game's burlap and wood maps under vertex colours (a cloth's colour is given as what it reads over the burlap, `dyed`), both drawn two-sided so they are one program; the straw, rope, thread and buttons are vertex colours alone. A sheaf of straws has a solid heart so it reads as a bundle from afar, and the glow of where it would stand (`scarecrowOutline`) is the wood and cloth alone: each straw a glow of its own added up to a glare. The skill's `warm()` returns one whole, so a first cast compiles nothing (`npm run lab -- cast strawman --class ranger --ab`). It shares no code with the training dummy: the dummy's straw is thinner, for the lobby's nearer camera. **Every straw grows from what it sticks out of** (from play: "floating hay pieces around the hat and body (that are not attached)"): the brim's fray is rooted on the brim's edge as the brim is bent there (`brimEdge`, which bends the hat's lathe too, `brimFray`: laid on a flat ring, it hung in the air where the brim waved up or was bitten and poked up through its top where it waved down), the neck's ruff at the collar's rim laid down the shoulders, and the collar's tuft in its opening (rooted inside the coat, both came out through its cloth as loose spikes). Look at it close with `npm run lab -- live`: a scarecrow stood where the hero stands (`live eval`, `buildScarecrow`), the hero hidden, pictured `--on` its head and hem from `front,left,right,back,above` at about 1 m.
