# `src/data/classes`

A file a class, registered in `index.ts`: its base stats, its skills (each skill's `impl` names a behaviour in `combat/skills/`, and every other field is tuning that behaviour reads), its model, its starter gear, its lobby accent, its loadout panel's title, its cast-point light and its item bases. Adding a class is in the root's Architecture.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

## Gotchas

- **Class balance is judged by damage a second as each class is played**, not by the data's numbers: a drawn shot's cooldown runs out before its draw starts, a fan takes its other four arrows from the quiver each time, a shower's arrows strike only the foe they fall on, and a fan's arrows a few metres apart miss a lone foe. The DPS probe (not in the repo: port it into the lab, a `dps` command, when it's needed again) played every damage skill alone for 15 s (a channel held, a drawn shot let go at full and at its least draw, anything else cast as soon as it's ready, energy kept full) and a rotation per class for 30 s on its own energy (cooldown skills when ready, a channel while the energy lasts, the basic attack between), against training dummies (they stand still and never die), one or a pack of eight, 2 m off and 9 m out, gear's stats left out. With the ranger's numbers raised: one target, the mage 201 a second, the warrior 260 up close, the ranger 239 (172 before); a pack, the mage 1234 at range, the warrior 797 round him, the ranger 958 at range (490). Gear multiplies the mage's damage more than the others' (three damage stats, all on his spells): `npm run lab -- gear` rolls full sets for each class at each tier, at epic item level 30 5.07 times against the warrior's 4.08 and the ranger's 3.90.
- **Health by class** (`data/classes/`, `npm run lab -- gear`): the mage has the least of his own (800, the ranger 1050, the warrior 1300) and gains the least from gear: a class's Health rolls are scaled by its `gearLife` (the ranger's 1.45, the warrior's 1.6) as an item is made, so at epic item level 30 a set gives the mage 1290, the ranger 1660 and the warrior 2058. Health Regeneration on gear is a share of the class's own (`STATS.lifeRegen`, `computeStats`), so it gives most to those that regenerate most (the warrior's 6 a second against the mage's 4). The healing draught heals a flat amount and a share of the most Health (`SkillDef.heal`, `healPct`: 190 and 30%), so a hero with little Health still gets a good draught. Items made before these keep what they rolled: an old flat regeneration roll now reads as a few per cent.
- **A mage's weapon has a school, which sets the spell the weapon itself casts** (#402, `ClassDef.schools`, `data/schools.ts`): arcane, fire, lightning or healing, each rolled a quarter of the time on any base (`makeItem`), the first the school of a weapon saved before there were schools (`attune` in `loot/profile.ts`) and of the starter staff. A skill with `school` is cast only through a weapon of that school; its key otherwise casts the spell of the school held, or with no weapon the class's `schools.bare` (`resolveFor` in `loot/loadout.ts`): the mage's left click is Splintering Bolt (arcane), Cinderburst (fire), Stormcall (lightning), Lifethread (healing), or Void Lance with no staff. Every other skill is the same with any staff. Each school keeps its own bar, and no staff another (`HeldStyle`). With no staff the lance is cast from either hand, a staff's cast, or from both with two of its keys held: half as much damage again, the Energy gone half as fast again (its `both`; `src/combat/skills/AGENTS.md`). The names and numbers are working ones, the owner's to settle; `npm run lab -- staffs --class mage` holds what each staff does, `lab tooltips` and `lab log` its figures.
