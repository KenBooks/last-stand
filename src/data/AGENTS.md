# `src/data`

Pure tuning data, typed against `types.ts`, with no behaviour: keep it so.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); each directory under this one has an AGENTS.md of its own.

- `balance.ts`: the global knobs (the camera, the arena, the run, block, the damage numbers' colours).
- `classes/`: the playable classes. `enemies.ts`: the foes (`ai` picks a behaviour in `entities/enemyAI.ts`, `model` a builder in `entities/models/`, `evade` how a shooter gets out of a hero's attack, `entities/evade.ts`; `throws` what a slammer throws at a hero too far off to slam, `entities/throws.ts`; `heft` the weight it puts up as a hero's charge rams it, `combat/ram.ts`; stats at wave 1, `SCALING` for their growth).
- `biomes.ts`: each biome's enemy pool, ladder of bosses (`bosses`: boss wave 1 the first, wave 2 the second, round again past its end, each time harder by `SCALING`; `bossOfWave` is what the run, the banner and the Continue button read), minimap, surfaces and music. A rung may bring its twin (`twin`: a second boss rising beside it, the two sharing one life, `EnemyDef.shares`, the target frame naming the pair, `EnemyDef.pair`; `rungBoss` names a twin's rung for the records). A rung may come as an encounter (`guards`: lesser bosses rising with it, each at its own `EnemyDef.asGuard` life and damage, warding it while they stand, rising at its spots where it has them (`at`): the Crypt's second rung, the Ossuary Cantor with two Bell-Wardens, rising across from each other on the crypt's ring walk). A new boss is a rung on a ladder, an `EnemyDef` with `boss` and usually a `bossKit` (`src/entities/AGENTS.md`), its behaviour and its model. `waves.ts`: how a wave is made from its budget, and the loot rules (`LOOT.rarityWeights`: a weight per rarity in `RARITIES`' order; Ascendant only from wave 8, whatever the wave's reward bonus, about 1 reward in 160 there and 1 in 70 by wave 15).
- `items.ts`: slots, rarities, stats and affixes. `quality.ts`: the graphics presets (none changes a shader). `tips.ts`: the loading screen's tips. `guide.ts`: the in-game guide's words, no figures (`src/ui/guide/AGENTS.md`). `music.ts`: where each track loops and how the music fades. `heroColours.ts`: the colours a player's hero can wear (`entities/models/dye.ts`). `bugReport.ts`: the kinds of trouble the bug report's form offers and the form's limits (`docs/agents/ui-menus.md`), which the server holds a report to as well (`server/traces/reports.mjs`: its kinds a copy of these ids, its tests holding the two alike). `heroLooks.ts`: each class's looks (a face of `models/face.ts` and its hair; the first his own) and the hair colours a player picks, kept in the profile (`docs/agents/heads.md`).

How a wave comes in is `src/game/AGENTS.md`; the quality presets, `src/core/AGENTS.md`; adding a class or a biome, the root's Architecture.
