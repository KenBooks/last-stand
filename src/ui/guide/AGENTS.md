# `src/ui/guide`

The in-game guide (`#guide` in `index.html`, styles `src/ui/styles/_guide.scss`): every foe and boss, each hero, and tips, browsed by section and entry. Opened by **G** anywhere (but over the hero selection screen or a run's summary), the pause menu's **Guide** tab (a door per section), and How to play's **Open the guide** (the notice board). The panels' lobby has no link of its own for it: its row of links is laid out in rows of three that keep the panel from scrolling (`docs/agents/ui-menus.md`'s bug report note), and G and the pause menu reach it there. It always opens over the pause menu (`showGuide` in `main.ts`): opened from elsewhere the menu is opened under it, holding the game as a pause does, and put away with it; Esc or G closes the guide first.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md); the UI's are [`src/ui/AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

- `index.ts`: the modal, its sections and entries, where it was left (`last-stand.guide-page` in local storage), and the dev build's warning of notes missing.
- `foes.ts`: a foe's or boss's page from `data/enemies.ts` (each foe's file in `data/foes/`; the list by where it's met, then the cheaper first, stats grown by `SCALING`, elites by `HERO`, a boss's fight by `BOSS_AI`, throws, evasion), `data/biomes.ts` (where and from which wave it comes), its blow against the hero's own armour or resistance now.
- `heroes.ts`: a class's page from its data: base figures, every skill as its tooltip states it (`skillTooltip`: the played hero's own figures, the others' base ones), the weapon styles (gear made from the class's own bases, each style shown where its default loadout differs: `STYLE_TRIES`), the staffs' schools, loadouts and combos, the gear bases per slot.
- `tips.ts`: banking, loot chances, items and the lobby floor, block, daze and freeze, staying alive, waves and score, co-op, duels (`PVP`, `DUEL_WINS` and `WINS_USUAL` in `game/duel.ts`), controls, each figure from `data/balance.ts`, `data/waves.ts`, `data/items.ts` and the classes' skills.
- `portraits.ts`: the library's Brother Hollis is pictured here too, first, for his talk's card (`ui/talk.ts`): another of him built on the library's book (`scholarBook`), posed looking up at the one he talks to, head and shoulders by a candle against a dim library (`scholarStaged`, `SCHOLAR_SHOT`; aimed at his head's joint, at his neck, the oval held his chest and cut his head off: it's aimed from the top of his cap). Every picture's meshes have their buffers sent a batch a piece before its draw (`BUFFERS_A_PIECE`, each batch drawn alone): sent by the draw, his held a frame up 72 ms (`lab guide`: 28 ms now, one mesh of his the most). Where the pictures aren't made behind the lobby (Low and Very low, `portraitsBehind`), his is made as his talk opens (`portrait` starts them, `stepPortraits` steps them while the card waits, `talkWaitsForPortrait` in `main.ts`), a spinner in the oval till then; a try that comes back empty is tried again (`retryPortrait`), and the card never shows a drawing in its place.
- `portraits.ts`: each foe's and hero's own model drawn into a picture by a small WebGL renderer of the guide's own (never the game's, which links no program for them), **made behind the lobby a piece a frame** (`game/behind.ts`, queued by `main.ts` with the heroes, after them; at
High and Medium alone, `portraitsBehind` in `data/quality.ts`: on a phone's WebGL each portrait's compile and pieces
held the lobby's frames up 130 to 950 ms, five times in 14 s, trace S307-J274, and at Low and Very low the guide makes
them as it opens, `startPortraits`, `lab guide --boot verylow`: every picture drawn 9.3 s after G, each page's own
within 47 ms of it, a spinner till then), and **first while the guide is open** (`stepPortraits` each frame it's open, from `main.ts`: `buildOnly` steps them alone, `WHILE_OPEN` of a frame, ahead of the heroes and whatever holds the lobby's building: the fight's warm-up, a run, a pause; the list's pictures first, `hurryPortraits`, the page's own before them, `hurryPortrait`): the renderer, each model (a hero's in his build's own pieces), its staging, its compile asked for (`compileAsync`, its commands flushed at once), its textures sent a piece each, and its draw, copied, once the card has drawn it (a fence looked at each frame: copied at once, the page's paint waited 135 to 214 ms for the card), into a canvas of its own (`made`) that every place showing it is drawn from: a place is a canvas (`pic` in `parts.ts`), filled the frame the picture is drawn, not an `<img>` waiting on a PNG's encoding. Up to `SIDE_BY_SIDE` are made at once, their compiles running side by side; the first wanted is started and its textures sent before another is started. While the browser compiles them and nothing else is left, the build leaves the queue and comes back as one is done, so nothing behind the lobby waits on it (`lab behind` waited 1200 frames for it otherwise). A production build keeps the pictures in IndexedDB for its own build (`CACHE`, keyed by `__BUILD__`, encoded by `toBlob` off the page's thread, nothing waiting on it), another build's let go; a dev build makes them afresh. Opening the guide makes nothing. A place waiting for its picture shows a gilt spinner (`.gd-pic.waiting`, still with reduced motion); one made already is drawn before the page is painted, with no spinner.
- The words are `src/data/guide.ts`: how and why, **never a figure** (every number is read from the data as a page opens).

## Keeping it current

**Any change that adds or changes a foe, boss, skill, weapon style, staff school, item base or mechanic updates the guide in the same PR.** The figures follow by themselves; the words don't:

- A new foe fails the typecheck until its file in `data/foes/` has its `note` (`FoeEntry`); `FOE_NOTES` is read from those files.
- A skill or class with no note still shows from its data, and the dev build's console warns (`guide: no notes in data/guide.ts for ...`) as the guide opens. Write its `CLASS_NOTES[cls].skills[impl]`, and add it to a loadout or combo where it belongs.
- A loadout or combo names skills by `impl` and shows only where the class has them all: notes for a skill still in an open PR can land before it, and appear as it merges.
- A new weapon style shows once its default loadout differs from the styles before it; give it a `STYLE_NOTES` entry (and a `STYLE_TRIES` gear set if none of them makes it). A new school needs a `SCHOOL_NOTES` entry.
- A behaviour change that the words describe (a foe that now dodges, a skill that now dazes) means rereading its note: the notes say "slow", "dazes", "cannot be knocked back".
- A number in a behaviour's code (as the boss's summons and volleys were) belongs in the data, where the guide can read it.

## Gotchas

- **Nothing heavy may run as the guide opens or changes page** (Victor on #665: "lags for 1-2sec, unacceptable"). Made all at once as it opened, its pictures held the page 1.2 s (`lab guide --canary`); one a frame, 90 to 110 ms a frame for a second. Made behind the lobby, the opening's frame is 12 to 17 ms and the longest moment of a walk through every page, its pictures still being made, 45 to 62 ms (a hero's first draw, which sends his geometry to the card). Check with `npm run lab -- guide` (and `--canary=stall`), and `npm run lab -- behind`, whose queue the pictures share.
- **Every picture must show soon after the guide opens** (from play: "icons not loading in guide"). Made behind the lobby alone, 2 ms a frame after the heroes and never while the fight's warm-up ran, a first visit's guide showed the Voidling's picture 20 s after it opened and most of the others after a minute, the rest empty frames. They're made first while it's open (`WHILE_OPEN`). Victor then: "model renders take 5-10sec to appear", and every placeholder needs a loading indicator. What's left of the wait is the browser's own compiles of the pictures' programs, in the guide's own WebGL context: in the lab's browser (below normal priority) on the busy shared PC they took up to 12 to 21 s, made one at a time 24 s for every picture; side by side, 8 to 10 s, and each page's picture drawn within about 20 ms of its compile's end. The first pictures pay for the programs the others share (most come in 10 to 50 ms after them). `lab guide` checks each page's own picture is drawn within 150 ms of its opening or of its compile's end, every picture within 20 s of G with none made before and the lobby's building held, and every waiting place's spinner (`--canary=slow`: made behind the lobby alone, which it must catch). Don't wait on a compile per picture in turn, and don't read a shader's log back (`checkShaderErrors` off): a read waits for the browser's other compiles. **A picture is drawn with small copies of its maps** (`smallTextures`, at most 256 px, made once per texture, swapped in for its draw alone and put back: the materials are the game's own): the game's textures are canvases up to 2048 px, and the guide's context read each back to send it, which held one frame 338 ms and another 706 on the main that brought the library (1.58 s once, `live`, the lobby's own building not held). With them small, the longest moment of `lab guide` on a quiet PC was 103 ms, the paint after the Sporecaller's first draw.
- No flowchart of a run's loop (Bank or Continue) and no boss seen from above: both were taken out from play; the loop is told in words on Tips' first page.

- Its figures read `G.player` (armour, resistance, speed, the hero's own skill figures): a page is made as it opens, so open it again to see a gear change.
- The foes' pictures take a frame or more each; under the lab's clock they come slowly (its frames hold `requestAnimationFrame`, and `compileAsync` polls on real time): run `live frames` a few hundred times before a picture.
- Checked at 1920x1080, 1024x700, 820x1180@2 and 832x384@2.8125 with `npm run lab -- live` (G pressed by `live eval`, a frame run before clicking its sections); its opening's cost by `npm run lab -- guide`.
