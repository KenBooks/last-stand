# Party portraits

Beside the portraits, each class has a default picture, `default-<class>.webp`: shown where a player chose no portrait (`../classOutlines.ts`), a bust in greys with no face (the mage's starred hood and staff, the warrior's closed helm, the ranger's feathered cap, face wrap and tall bow), painted by Codex's image generation from the brief kept with its originals in the main checkout's `.tmp/outline-art/` (`round1.md`'s `mage-a` and `warrior-a`, the `-b` the same as carved reliefs; `round2.md`'s `ranger-c`), made grey, lifted a little (gamma 0.78, to read at 36 px on the cards' dark) and saved at 128 px, WebP quality 82, 3 to 4 KB each. **No two classes' may share an outline at 36 px**: the ranger's first was a hood over a dark face, as the mage's is (from the owner: "archer needs to look very different from mage, they look similar currently"), so it wears a cap and holds its bow the picture's height; judge a new class's shrunk to 36 px beside the others. They are inlined in the bundle (`?inline`), 11 KB in all, so they show offline and make no request; keep one under 6 KB.

The repository contains exactly fifteen portraits, all 192 px square WebP, quality 82: five per class (IDs 11–15), arranged in one row with blue, red, green, purple, and ochre outfits. Keep the IDs stable so saved choices remain valid. Each class's five choices are direct crops from the owner's sheets: Ranger from Cinematic Fantasy Hero Portraits, Mage from Five Elemental Mages Lineup, and Warrior from Five Warriors, One Banner. Frames and margins are excluded; `prompts.json` records only these fifteen portraits, with their source and square crop. These are an explicit binary-asset exception. Original PNGs, previous exports and prompts are preserved locally in `.tmp/portrait-art/`; retired unused exports are archived in its `retired/` directory, not tracked. Regenerate/convert with `tools/portraits`. Portrait URLs use `?url&no-inline`, never eager Image creation. Only picker opening or a selected party portrait fetches images, and the service worker excludes them from install precaching. Keep them out of name tags and chat.
