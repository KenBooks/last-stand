# Party portraits

Beside the portraits, each class has a default picture, `default-<class>.webp`: shown where a player chose no portrait (`../classOutlines.ts`). It is an outline: a head-and-shoulders bust in one bold pale-silver line with a few inner lines, over a flat dark grey fill on near black, no face (the mage's pointed hood, star and crystal staff, the warrior's great helm, pauldrons and sword hilt, the ranger's feathered cap, bow and arrows). What the owner asked of them, in turn: flat SVG shapes were not "fancy, like proper triple A quality"; painted busts in greys made the ranger and the mage two hoods alike at 36 px ("archer needs to look very different from mage") and the ranger's repaint was "anatomically wrong" (a small head turned a quarter on a front-facing chest); so "outline only", which "needs to be recognizable", and with the grey fill, not the line alone ("with grey will looks better when zoomed out"). **Judge one shrunk to 36 px, unlabelled, beside the others**: a reviewer told nothing must name each class. Drawn by Codex's image generation from the briefs kept with the originals in the main checkout's `.tmp/outline-art/` (`round4.md`, its `-fill` pictures), made grey and saved at 128 px, WebP quality 86, 3.6 to 4.6 KB each. They are inlined in the bundle (`?inline`), 12 KB in all, so they show offline and make no request; keep one under 6 KB.

The repository contains exactly fifteen portraits, all 192 px square WebP, quality 82: five per class (IDs 11–15), arranged in one row with blue, red, green, purple, and ochre outfits. Keep the IDs stable so saved choices remain valid. Each class's five choices are direct crops from the owner's sheets: Ranger from Cinematic Fantasy Hero Portraits, Mage from Five Elemental Mages Lineup, and Warrior from Five Warriors, One Banner. Frames and margins are excluded; `prompts.json` records only these fifteen portraits, with their source and square crop. These are an explicit binary-asset exception. Original PNGs, previous exports and prompts are preserved locally in `.tmp/portrait-art/`; retired unused exports are archived in its `retired/` directory, not tracked. Regenerate/convert with `tools/portraits`. Portrait URLs use `?url&no-inline`, never eager Image creation. Only picker opening or a selected party portrait fetches images, and the service worker excludes them from install precaching. Keep them out of name tags and chat.
