# `src/combat/skills`

Each skill's behaviour, registered by its `impl` name in `index.ts` (`SKILL_IMPLS`) and referenced from class data. `types.ts` is the contract: an instant skill has `cast()`, a channel `start/tick/stop` with a typed state object, and `Needs<'field'>` requires optional `SkillDef` tuning fields.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

- **The warrior:** `cleave.ts` (Rending Cleave), `crescent.ts` (Thunder Crescent), `powerStrike.ts`, `twinFangs.ts`, `bullRush.ts`, `raiseShield.ts`, `tempest.ts` (Steel Tempest), `ironBellow.ts`; `slash.ts` draws the blade arcs Thunder Crescent, Steel Tempest and Bull Rush throw or spin; with both hands empty, `knuckles.ts` (Bare Knuckles), `heave.ts`, `livingFlail.ts`, `groundbreaker.ts` (His fists, below). A cut draws no arc of its own: its mark is the trail its blade leaves (`fx/swingTrail.ts`), and an arc laid flat at its tip's height drew a second one, level across the view while the cut through the eyes ran down it.
- **The mage:** `splinterBolt.ts`, `voidLance.ts`, `glacialNova.ts`, `starfall.ts`, `maelstrom.ts` (its look is `fx/firestorm.ts`), `blink.ts` (Veilstep); and the staffs' own spells by school, `cinderburst.ts` (fire), `stormcall.ts` (lightning, its arc `fx/stormArc.ts`), `lifethread.ts` (healing).
- **The ranger:** `ranger.ts` (the bow's shots and arrows), `arrowRain.ts` (Hailfletch), `quarryMark.ts`, `strawman.ts`.
- **Shared:** `potion.ts` (the healing draught), `readiness.ts` (a held skill's preview while a release would cast nothing).

A skill runs on every game for its visuals: its damage goes through `hitEnemy` and its hits on players through `hurtPlayer` (the root's Co-op rule). Each of its hits passes its own `def` as `skill`, which the battle log lists it under (`npm run lab -- log` holds every skill's hits to it). A skill with materials of its own returns sample meshes from its `warm()` hook (the root's shader rule). An area skill calls `hurtPropsIn` next to its `hitEnemy` loop (Breakable props in `src/world/AGENTS.md`). Its effect is held to the light budget: its materials wrapped in `nearGlow()`, and measured with `npm run lab -- cast <skill>` (Bright effects in `src/core/AGENTS.md`). A held skill gives its preview a `Readiness` and a reader in the lab's `held`. Its icon: `src/ui/skillArt/AGENTS.md`.

A skill that deals damage says how in `hits(def, stats)` (`SkillHit` in `types.ts`), beside the code that deals it: each kind of hit's base, its type and tags, how many a second for a channel's ticks, the least share of it a hit falls to (a crystal's edge, a draw let go early) and a note (a split's count, the full draw's bonus). Its tooltip states the hero's figures from it (`src/ui/AGENTS.md`), so a change to how a skill deals damage changes its `hits` with it: `npm run lab -- tooltips` casts each skill at foes stood where it strikes, with the hero's damage, crit and casting speed raised, and holds every hit it lands to the range its tooltip states, a channel's pace to the one it states, and the figures to a weapon's damage changed (`--canary` raises a skill's damage behind its tooltip's back, which it must catch).

## Gotchas

- **Killing blows drive the bodies** (`combat/fling.ts`, `entities/corpse.ts`). Each hit says how its blow drives what it kills (`blow`):
  - a sweep along its swing (`swingWay`, the way its blade trail shows it: `model.swing`);
  - an arrow on along its flight (`arrowBlow`);
  - a blast out and up from where it struck;
  - a slam down into the floor (Power Strike).

  A blow that knows where across the body it struck says so (`on`), and off the body's middle it turns the body as it throws it: an arrow or a bolt where it hit, Void Lance's beam where it passes the body, a sweep's blade on the body's near side (`swingOn`: pushed there along the swing, the body turns with its front carried on along the blade's way). A blast, a chop or a charge strikes through the middle and turns nothing.

  The bodies already lying are moved too. A blast throws them (`blastCorpses`), each once a cast where a cast strikes again and again (`once`: Starfall's crystals each threw the same bodies, and one flew 17 m; Thunder Crescent's wave); a body the same blow killed is thrown by its own fling. A channel or a storm that holds the fallen adds a field while it lasts (`addField`, taken away in `stop`): Void Lance carries them along its beam, and Maelstrom whirls them round it and burns them. `npm run lab -- death` follows each skill's kills.
- **Bull Rush carries what it meets** (`bullRush.ts`, `entities/carry.ts`, tuned by `CARRY` in `data/balance.ts`). Each foe it meets (within 1.1 m of its middle past the foe's size, or touching one it carries, a frame's way on ahead too: met as it was overlapped, a foe was shoved its overlap at once) is struck once and carried before the hero to the stop, stacked with the others along the charge, its body kept clear of his (`carryEnemy` in `combat/damage.ts`, judged as a hit is: only where `dealsDamage(by)`), then thrown on ahead through the air, fanned out by where across the charge it rode, and dazed where it lands; an elite rides too but is shoved on, not thrown, a boss is struck and never carried (it stops the charge, #397), and a training dummy stands. A kill's body, and any body lying in its way, rides before it on its field (`ploughOf`) and is thrown on as the living are as it stops (`throwPloughed`, `Shove.set`: set going so, not added to its ride, as the charge stops from a third of its speed: added, a body slid 15 m on). Thrown aside as it was met, every foe it ran on past ended behind or beside the hero as he stopped (0.8 to 5.4 m behind, the owner's "enemies falling behind me at the end of bull rush looks lame"). The stop strikes nothing. `npm run lab -- rush` follows each foe it meets, in a lane it finds clear of props (one before the stop, a log, held two thrown foes at its side).
- **Bull Rush is weighed down by what it carries, and staggers him after a crowd** (#397, #560, `SkillDef.push` and `ram`, `combat/ram.ts`; from play: "need to nerf warrior, he should not be able to push around big enemies, also big groups of enemies should be pushed not too far, depending on % damage modifier (gear)", "ramming into a large group of enemies should briefly stun the character. Currently it's too OP", and the owner's "you should not be able to push around so many enemies and bosses. The player should be stunned a bit, but don't make it too powerful, it should still be powerful"). Each foe its blow meets weighs its kind's `heft` (`EnemyDef.heft`: 1, the Brute and the Hulk 2.5, a boss 6), an elite twice that (`ram.elite`). **The load:** the living it carries weigh on it, lightened by the hero's All Damage % (`push.gear` 0.5: at +100% the load is two thirds; with nothing carried it goes its whole 9 m whatever the gear, `loadOf`); each weight takes 7% of its speed (`push.slow`, `paceOf`, his dash's speed scaled from its own each frame), and at 6 (`push.most`: six common foes, three elites, two slammers and a foe) it stops where it is (`Player.haltDash`, told to the partners as `halt`), all it carries thrown on from there. A boss is struck but never carried or shoved, and stops it at once: he stops a metre short of its body. A charge through three common foes still goes 7.7 m of its 9 (`lab rush`), and seven packed together stop it at the first, 2.1 m on. **The stagger:** every foe met counts for it, a kill at half (`ram.killed`: what dies gives little, so damage shortens it). From 5 of weight (`at`: five common foes, two slammers, an elite slammer or a boss) he's staggered from the stop for 0.4 s (`least`), rising evenly to 0.7 s at 9 (`most`, `full`: a pack that stopped it with the dead it ploughed), held there; with a shield 0.3 to 0.5 s (its style's `ram`: braced behind it). Under 5 nothing, so a short charge still moves him freely. Staggered (`Player.stun`, `stunned`: one of `staggered`'s two causes, the other a broken guard), he can't move, attack or cast, what he was casting or holding is let go, and he reels in the stagger's pose with a dazed foe's stars over his head (`fx/dazed.ts`; not through his own eyes), a grunt and a thud (`sfx.bullDazed`); a Twin Fangs cut falling due in it lands nothing. The load, the stop and the stagger are his own game's (it moves him and judges his hits: on a partner's game the foes met are its own copies), told to the others as `halt` and `stun`. The tooltip says both trade-offs with the figures the weapons held give (`skillRamHTML` in `ui/skillDamage.ts`). `npm run lab -- rush --scenarios stun` rams a big pack, a small one, a killed one, four elites, a boss and a big pack with a shield (`page/rushStun.ts`; `--canary` cuts the big pack's stagger short, lifts the elites' most out of reach and carries the boss).
- **Skills are cast with Bull Rush** (the owner's "charge a power strike, then bull rush, and land a power strike during bull rush"): it's cast over another skill being cast or held, which goes on and lands as it would (`SkillDef.overCast`: the owner's "allow bull rush while in steel tempest", the spin going on through the charge), and its dash is open (`DashOpts.open`): the hero's other skills are cast as it runs and as it holds at its stop, all but a charge or a blink (`dashes` on a skill's behaviour: Bull Rush, Veilstep). Twin Fangs' lunge cast as the charge runs rides on it (`Player.startDash`, `DashState.ride`, the owner's "twin fangs should be allowed too during bull rush"): its cuts posed over the charge's legs for the lunge's time, its own way given up (put in the charge's place, it cut the charge short where it was cast), held on standing for the rest of its time if the charge ends first; nothing else is cast as it rides, as nothing is in its own lunge. Under the charge the spin and the lunge leave the legs and the drop to it (`models/warrior.ts`: their own stances left the feet 21 to 27 cm off its strides).
- **Twin Fangs' two cuts come 0.15 s apart** (`FANGS` in `data/balance.ts`, which the skill's lunge and strikes and the model's cuts both read; `lab --tune twinFangs.apart=...`): at 0.27 and 0.63 s they were the owner's "too slow", slower than a dual wielder's basic attack, a cut every 0.3 s ("both hits in a rapid succession", "faster than regular LMB"). `npm run lab -- hits twinFangs --than cleave --apart 0.2 --gear dual` (in `check`) holds them to it. Through the eyes each blade fades in from rest over 0.4 of its cut (`warriorFangs.in`): quicker, an axe's and a mace's head jumped 21 cm in a frame. A cast as the charge runs keeps the body on its way, not turned to the aim (`Player.dashRunning`), and the model poses the skill over the charge's legs (`ActionState.under`, `docs/agents/heroes.md`). A partner's copy marks only a skill's own cast landed as it replays its landing (`Player.replay`: the charge's landing marked the strike under it landed, and on the partner's screen its build-up stopped short). `npm run lab -- rush` casts skills with it (`page/rushCasts.ts`), `coop --scenarios strike` on both screens.
- **Steel Tempest's look** (`tempest.ts`, #573): three blade trails a third of a turn apart at three heights (the first on the blade, the others thin streaks), a faint ring on the floor at the skill's reach, wind streaming off it and dust kicked up at each strike; let go, it runs down over 0.3 s (`WIND_DOWN`, an effect of its own) rather than vanishing. Every part is one program, the blade arcs' (`slash.ts`, whose `uTrail` sets how long a sweep's tail is), and nothing pulses with the strikes: the long tails overlap round the circle, so a patch the whirl turns past keeps about the same light (`lab cast tempest --gear twoHanded`: no flash in top-down or third person). Followers as wide as the blade's trail made a milky disc over the foes in reach.

The first foe his hands meet that can be lifted (`liftable`: no boss, no elite, no training dummy, none bigger than `FLAIL.lifts`, so not a Cinder Brute or a Barkhide Hulk) is seized and swung round him, striking every foe in `radius` each 3 rad of the turn and taking `FLAIL.bare` of a strike itself as it strikes. Swung into a thing standing in its way (a rock, a column, a log: an obstacle `FLAIL.stands` m high or more, along the line of its body), it takes that blow too, with the turn's next strike (from play: "when they are in a spin (1), and they hit objects, like rocks or colums, the mobs should take damdge"; one a strike, as a rock is several circles and each took one; a breakable prop is chipped). - **Cooldowns are per skill id**, shared by every key the skill is bound to (the same spell may sit on several keys).
- **Gear-driven skills and block:** `Player.gear` comes from the equipped items: weapon base, two-handed (`ClassDef.twoHanded`), shield (an off-hand with Block Amount), and a weapon in the off-hand (`ClassDef.dualWield`: a one-hander fits the off-hand slot, and its implicit counts at `OFFHAND_WEAPON`). Each weapon style (shield, two-handed, dual, one-handed) has its own loadout (`loot/loadout.ts` `weaponStyle`); changing style loads that style's, and a style not set up yet starts from the class defaults fitted to the gear. With two weapons the model's swings alternate hands, and `tip` moves to the swinging weapon. A two-hander is held in both hands, the left one following its lower grip by arm IK (`fistReach` in `models/hands.ts`); a pose that takes that grip out of the left arm's reach (the war cry's flung arms; Steel Tempest held it in one hand until it was placed by the weapon, `docs/agents/heroes.md`) lets go with it, the arm easing back to the pose's own, rather than leaving the hand floating off the wrist. `Model.setGear` shows it, and `profile.equipFromStash` never lets a two-hander and an off-hand be equipped together (an off-hand equipped puts the two-hander back in the stash); the lobby shows a two-hander's empty off-hand locked, a padlock whose tooltip names the weapon taking both hands and, for a class with off-hands, says what equipping one does. A skill with `needs: 'shield'` (or `'twoHanded'`) gives way to its `fallback` for the gear held (`Player.skillAt`), and the lobby dims it in the skill book while the gear doesn't allow it (`Player.usable`). Blocking is in `Player.tryBlock`: a per-hit chance with a recovery (`BLOCK` in `data/balance.ts`), or every frontal hit while a skill with `block` is channelled, until a hit bigger than that breaks the guard and staggers the player (`guardBroken`, `staggered`: move only; a Bull Rush's stun is the other stagger, `stunned`, no move either). Melee skills draw their trail through the weapon tip (`skills/cleave.ts` `swingArc`), so keep the model's swing timing in step with it; the weapon itself leaves a white trail wherever it's swung (`fx/swingTrail.ts`, `Model.swung`: a new strike's action is one of the warrior model's `STRIKES`), which needs nothing of a skill. A skill's `styles` tunes it for a weapon style over its own (`loot/loadout.ts` `forStyle`, applied by `Player.fitToGear` as the gear changes, which the lab's `--gear` calls too: set by the gear alone, a two-hander swung at a one-hander's pace). Rending Cleave swings slower and harder with a two-hander (0.6 s, 92) and quicker and lighter with two blades (0.3 s, 45), each about as much damage and energy a second as with one, and Steel Tempest spins slower with one (`spin`, 10.5 rad/s against 15: it strikes once each 3 rad round, so each strike is harder, the same damage a second). **Bull Rush strikes a quarter harder with a shield** (150 against 120, #398, from play: "bull rush with shield just adds damage, rest same"): its blows, and its stagger after a crowd a quarter shorter (0.3 to 0.5 s against 0.4 to 0.7), its reach, speed, carry and cost the same; a quarter, as a shield's hero gives up a great weapon's weight and a second blade's pace for his block, and less than a two-hander adds to a cleave's blow. A style's `styleNote` says what it adds in the tooltip's line of what the figures are made of (`Base 150 per foe (+25% with a shield)`, `ui/skillDamage.ts`). The HUD's and the lobby's tooltips show the skill as it is with the weapons held; `lab tooltips` holds each style's figure to the one-hander's times its tuning's share, and `lab rush --scenarios bonus` the charge's blows with a shield and without to its data.

- **Drawn shots** (`SkillDef.draw`, the Ranger's mouse skills and Lancewood Shot): drawn while the key is held (`draw` s to full), loosed when it's let go but never before `minDraw` of that; `castTime` is then what follows the release (the follow-through and the next arrow). `Player.tryCast` starts one with no end, `loose` gives it one, and `fire` passes how far it was drawn (`power`) to the skill's `cast`; the pose reads `ActionState.draw`, `loosed`, `pitch` (the skill's `pitch` hook: the arc for the arrow as it would leave), `yaw`, `arrows` and `fan`. A drawn shot pressed for while the hero is busy (a tap during the last one's reload) is queued and drawn as soon as he's free, in the frame the last one ends (started the next frame, the pose had a frame with no shot between them and the bow stepped out to rest and back), loosed at once if its key is up again: tapping fires least-draw shots one after another. A bow's hero (`ClassDef.quiver`) keeps an arrow on the string between shots (`Player.nocked`: on once taken from the quiver, gone with the shot, back on as the cast's reload ends, so a cut reload leaves none) and puts it back after `idle` s without a shot; a bow's shot with none on the string takes one from the quiver first (`CastState.nockT`, `fetch` s, before the draw's time and the least draw count; the model gets `ActionState.fetch`), and co-op's `cast` carries it (`n`). Pressing or casting any other skill forgets it. Touch holds a drawn bow while the button is held and looses as it's let go. Co-op: the `cast` action carries `h` (held: `dur` is the time to full draw) and `fire` carries `k`, the draw. Arrows (`skills/ranger.ts`) fly as fast as the draw sent them (a bow drives its arrow about in proportion to the pull, `pullAt`), hit as hard as the energy that gives (the pull's square), on the lower ballistic arc that crosses the target at a man's chest (`launchPitch`), turning to follow their path; they pass over a foe they're above (`ProjectileOpts.level`). A projectile's `tick` may end its flight. A drawn shot's `charging` hook runs every frame of its draw with how far it's drawn: every bow's shot glints once it's drawn to full (`drawGlint`: motes of light drawn in onto the arrowhead until it's loosed, with the bow's ready sound as it comes to full, and nothing as it's drawn, so the light says it's full: a burst alone and nothing after read as the charge gone; `fx/glintMotes.ts`, specks a few pixels across drawn by one quad on the arrowhead, as glow particles fade within 3.5 m of the camera and through the eyes nothing of them showed; a star of light there read as a bright spark, too bright in every view), and in the close views the crosshair is bold (`Player.drawnFull`, `#crosshair.full`: swelling into it once as a heart knocks, about its own middle, never pulsing on, none with reduced motion; on a layer of its own, the swell the compositor's; centred by `transform`, it swelled about where its box was before that and shifted up and left, so it's centred by `translate`), the hit hardest at full draw (from `FULL_DRAW` of the draw, Lancewood Shot's harder still, its `fullBonus`). Measure with `npm run lab -- draw --class ranger` (`--canary`). While the local hero draws one, a thin red line previews each arrow's path if it were loosed now (`fx/aimLaser.ts`, from `flightPath` in `skills/ranger.ts`: the arrow's own flight stepped as the projectile steps it, a frame at 60 fps, moving and then falling, with its collisions: the ground, the wall as it's drawn (`world/wall.ts`: over it, the trees beyond), a prop it doesn't clear, the first foe unless it pierces, over a foe it's above by its height at the step's end; stepped otherwise, a grazing arrow's hit came out the other way), with a dot where it strikes: a fixed few pixels wide at any distance (widened in screen space in its vertex shader, so not `nearGlow`-dimmed: it never fills the view), faded near the camera and eased in and out (never flashing), only on its own player's screen. Measure it against the shots (a fixed-clock probe: the preview's ends read from its meshes in the frame the shot is loosed, against where each arrow ended, every skill, draw, target and view: within a frame's flight) and the share of the first-person view's middle it covers.
