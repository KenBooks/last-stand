# `src/entities`

- A local playable spare must reapply `ownColour()` when `Player.unstowed` brings it back. Its constructor may have run before the player changed colour or joined/left a room; keeping its old dye made a cached class switch lose the current colour. Apply after gear/stat reset so skinned and item material copies are present. `lab heropick` changes the saved colour after the spare is prepared, then chooses it; its colour-loss canary must be detected.

The things that move and fight.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); each directory under this one has an AGENTS.md of its own.

- `player.ts`: the player character: input to movement and skills, resources, damage, block, animation, and the remote players' copies in co-op. `movement.ts`: how a hero's body moves over the ground, by pushing against it with its feet.
- `foes/`: a file a foe (or a pair): what it adds to the game as it loads (`foeKit.ts`: `foeModels`, `foeFx`, `foeAI`; its fx names declared in `FoeFx`, its ai kind in `types.ts`'s `EnemyAIKinds`, its attacks in `combat/damage.ts`'s `EnemyAttacks`, each by `declare module`), loaded by `allFoes.ts`; its data is `data/foes/` (a new foe is a file in each, and touches no shared list). Its own sounds are `core/sounds/<name>.ts`.
- `bosses/`: a boss's file (or a pair's, `twins.ts`), as `foes/`'s are, but a chunk of its own loaded at its wave and no sooner (from the owner: "if its not boss wave, why load the boss info"): `bosses.ts` loads it (`loadFoe`, `isLazyFoe`: a foe whose `data/foes/` file has a namesake here, `foeLoaded`), and `game/bossReady.ts` loads and warms it under the fight's bar as Continue leads into its wave. Anything that builds a boss's model loads it first (the guide's portraits, the dev panel's spawn); a model asked for before its file loaded throws, saying so. The lab's own boot loads and warms every boss with every need (`__dev.hurry`), so a command that spawns one finds it ready; `npm run lab -- bosswave` boots cold and plays the real way in (`--canary`).
- `enemy.ts`: an enemy: movement, status effects, actions, damage, death (its decisions are `enemyAI.ts`, its look `models/`). `enemyAI.ts`: the behaviours (each sets a velocity and a facing and starts timed actions; what an attack looks like goes through `fx`, which co-op guests replay). `spawner.ts`: a foe spawned with its summoning effect.
- `sitting.ts`: an idle hero going to sit on a log or a stone nearby, or on the floor by a fire (`world/seats.ts`), sitting and getting up again (`Player.sitting`; posed by `models/sit.ts`).
- `evade.ts`: a foe getting out of a hero's attack it sees coming (`EnemyDef.evade`): ducked into the ground or blinked to a spot nearby, at random (`chance`), rolled once an attack (`evades`, called by the skills as their attack reaches a foe).
- `bossKit.ts`: the shared boss toolkit, so a new boss is mostly data (`EnemyDef.bossKit`) and its model: phases by its life's share (entered in order where the fight is simulated, every game told through enemyAI's `bossPhase` fx: its banner, its cue, `Enemy.bossPhase`; its own gameplay in `BossHooks.enter`/`exit`), telegraphed attacks (`telegraphed`: an action whose hit lands as its `warn` ends, its model told the share through `ActionState.hit` from the data, so a guest's copy has it; a ring on the floor where it has one), weak-spot windows (`openWeak`: `taken` times the damage from every hero's hit, `damageTaken` read by `hitEnemy` beside the marks; its aura in the window's colour at a beat a second). Nothing in it walks: a rooted boss uses it as one that shuffles. A boss may seize a hero, carrying him and lifting him off the ground (`Player.seize`, `lift`). ## Gotchas

- The local hero's two lights (`heroLights`) belong to the scene across hero changes, including cached spares. `Player.dispose` removes the body alone. Removing the shared lights while disposing an unused spare or the outgoing hero changes the scene's light count; a cached replacement does not run its constructor again, so its first draw recompiles the scene's shaders. `lab behind` checks spare disposal and the actual cached selection, then draws the lobby and requires zero new programs.

- `viewModel.ts` hides body meshes when the camera comes within 0.8 m of the neck, before first-person blending finishes. Waiting for `viewSettled()` alone exposes the inside of the head during slow entry. Arm placement still begins only once the view settles (`lab dev --cold`). **Only while the camera glides into the eyes or out of them** (`eyesInGlide` in `core/renderer.ts`): over the shoulder by a wall it comes in over his head and stays within 0.5 m of his neck, and hidden by nearness alone his head and body went there, his arms and weapons left, as a broken view through the eyes (from play in the hall's door, #601; `lab village --only cameraBeforeWalls,cameraOutsideHim`, its canary `hideBodyNearCamera`).

- **Hostile bolts leave the caster's tip** (`fireBolt` in `enemyAI.ts` sends `model.tip`'s height with the `bolt` fx), then settle to the level `BOLT_Y` over their first 2.2m: hits and the clear-shot check (`lineClear`) assume that level. A caster's cast pose should still hold its tip near bolt height (the Sporecaller holds its pod out at the target), so the settling is only a few centimetres.
- **An enemy's blow lands when its animation says so:** the data's `windup` is the one clock. The AI fires the strike, slam or shot at `windup / (windup + recover)` of the action, and `Enemy.animate` hands the same share to the model (`ActionState.hit`, from the data, so a co-op guest's copy has it too). A model's `attack` / `slam` brings the blow down to connect *at* `hit` (raise until just before it, strike ending on it, recover after) and a caster's build-up peaks on it, so a new enemy's animation is written against `a.hit`, never against fixed shares of the action. The dodge window is unchanged: `meleeStrike` still checks range and facing when it fires.
- **The shooters get out of the heroes' attacks** (`evade.ts`, `EnemyDef.evade`; the owner's words: the mushroom shooters "should duck down to a mushroom with a 50% chance, so the shots will miss, unless fired under their feet", the wraiths "teleport to a near location with 50% chance, escaping the shot", "from all ranged attack, including warrior crescent wave, and against bull rush too", and "only if player is in front of them... if another player shoots from behind, they cannot see that"). Each attack a foe sees is rolled once: a shot as it comes within `SEEN_AHEAD` (0.25 s) of the foe's body (`Projectile.foresee`), a cast's shots as one (`ProjectileOpts.judged`: judged arrow by arrow, a foe got out of every five-arrow volley), a wave, a charge or a beam as it reaches the foe, an area coming down as it first would strike it (Starfall's whole cast's, Hailfletch's channel's). It sees only an attack from a hero within `sees` (45°) either side of its facing, and only up and about (not rising, dazed, frozen, carried or already evading). The Sporecaller ducks (`EVADE_HOW.duck`) from shots alone: for `down` s it thinks of nothing, and its body sinks into the ground but for its cap (`Enemy.sunk`, drawn by its crowd's matrices: no mesh is hidden), over `DUCK.down`. As hits have it, it's down from the moment it ducks (`duckedTop`: a Lancewood Shot crosses 7 m in five frames, and met the body half down), struck only within its circle under its `top` (0.5 m, `strikesBody`'s `strikesDucked`): its skinned body is met by its hulls, which leave the cap's top out, and an arrow at the cap flew through. A shot aimed at its feet strikes it; one at a man's chest, a seeking bolt and Void Lance's beam pass over. The Rift Witch blinks (`EVADE_HOW.blink`) from every one of a hero's ranged attacks: to a spot `reach` (3 to 5.5 m) off it it could walk to in a line, clear of the props, the wall, every hero and the attack (`blinkSpot`, `inThreat`: off a line's sides or behind its front, out of an area's circle), torn out of the air where it stood and formed again there (`uDissolve` run back over `BLINK_FORM`, per foe in its crowd). A cast's other shots pass it by.
