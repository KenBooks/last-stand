# `src/game`

- A first visit's hall has no ordinary lobby frames to build playable spares. Shader readiness alone let an alternate hero be chosen while `swapHero` still had to construct a whole `Player` inside the click; repeat/same-hero picks avoided it. Hero warm-up parts now prepare their playable spare in slices before announcing 100% readiness (`Part.ready`, `prepareSpareHero`). Clamp shader-only progress below 1 until that finishes. `lab heropick` chooses a different hero on the initial screen and fails if the picking event completes any body; it previously tested only the current hero.

- Before Change hero opens, `prepareHeroSelection` waits for heads at the current detail and builds stale hall/spare heroes in safe slices. `warmingRuns` holds Auto and ordinary background stepping during preparation; the lobby remains interactive. Auto also holds while the hall is open: its staged three-hero scene isn't play's workload, and a trial there invalidated the prepared spare before choosing it. Background jobs retain their queued detail and wait for its workers in `buildBehind`. Worker preparation also precedes the warm-up's `makeHeadAhead` path. Never resolve a missing detail by sculpting a head in the opening click or the first frame after Back: this blocked input until queued key events arrived together. `lab behind` changes detail with the hall open, goes Back without a choice, and repeats the opening; `npm run stalls -- --scenario heroes --seconds 18 --auto low@518400 --js` traces the real browser sequence.

The run and what goes round it.

- `remapWaveSpawns` retargets queued units, the waiting boss and units already gate-scheduled, without restarting a run. Gate-scheduled units remain in `pendingUnits` until their callback runs; cleanup clears it. The dev biome switch uses this so an old-biome foe cannot appear after a swap.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- `run.ts`: the run: its waves, scoring, loot rolls, and the choice after every wave to bank or go on (the core loop, the root's Product rules). In co-op the host's game runs it for the party and the guests follow its reports.
- `warmup.ts`, `warm.ts`: the warm-up at boot and after it (what's made and handed to the engine before the lobby shows, what's built ahead, what warms behind the lobby or under a bar, and what each was measured against): [`docs/agents/warm-up.md`](../../docs/agents/warm-up.md), read before changing either, or the boot's order in `main.ts`.

## Gotchas

- **The run's end** (`checkRunOver` in `main.ts`): solo, the summary's button goes back to the lobby. In co-op every game goes back by itself once the run is over for everyone and its own summary has been shown, 2.5 s after the run's end is said (from play: "once the run ends and no one is playing any wave it should automatically spawn you back with the others"). A summary shown before the run ended (its player watching the rest, `RunState.watched`) goes with the run (the "You Have Fallen" panel stayed up after it); the one the run's end brought (the last out) stays over the lobby until closed (`enterMenu(keepSummary)`: its spoils still to read). A player out of the run stands down (`Player.standDown`: what he was drawing, casting or aiming let go, and no keys taken: a banked ranger drew and aimed on, his bow's sight drawn), and is never put down again by the host's word of his fall coming after he left (`net/sync.ts`). **The host's game stays in the run until it is over for everyone** (`runsOthersFight` in `net/role.ts`): out of it and watching, he is offered no Leave the run (the pause menu says he goes back with his partners), where a guest out of it may still go to the lobby ahead of the rest; his game runs their fight, and held a second time the button took it to the lobby, the guests left in a fight that had stopped, no wave to come and no end to send them back. - **A party meets more** (`PARTY` and `partyScale` in `data/balance.ts`, read by `buildWave` and `spawnGroup` for the heroes in the run as the wave starts, `r.party`): a wave's foes (its budget), the life on each (and on a boss's summons) and on a boss, and how many may stand at once above the wave's own most (`WAVES.maxAlive`, never past its cap of 38, the crowd's frame budget).
