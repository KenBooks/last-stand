# `tools/lab`

The lab as an agent works with it: what every command measures, in a line here and in full in [`COMMANDS.md`](COMMANDS.md); how to write a measure and what keeps it repeatable are [`WRITING.md`](WRITING.md), how the lab works [`WORKINGS.md`](WORKINGS.md). `README.md` is the short guide for people. The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); `node/`, `page/`, `test/` and `examples/` have an AGENTS.md of their own.

## Rules

- **Every harness is permanent.** A measure is a lab command from its first use (a module in `page/`, an entry in `page/commands.ts`, one in `node/commands.mjs`, its options in `node/options.mjs` and its entry in `COMMANDS.md`), or part of one that measures the same thing, never a probe left in `.tmp`.
- **A probe never takes the pointer.** Measure through the lab, whose browser stubs pointer lock, keyboard lock and fullscreen (a measure of mouse look has the page play the lock, `lab.pointerLock`); a page of your own grabbed the user's mouse.
- **A check run often takes minutes**, not half an hour (Don't waste compute in the root's Workflow): `--profile` it, cut what its measures don't read, run side by side what doesn't depend on each other, and while iterating run only the scenarios a change can touch.
- **While working on the village, run what the change can touch: `npm run lab -- village --since origin/main`** (`node/villageSince.mjs`: the checks by the files changed, committed or not; a file it can't place runs them all, a note none). The whole suite, `npm run lab -- check --commands village`, runs once before a push, never for each change (from the owner: "can we at least avoid running whole check for every change ?"). **It takes about 3 minutes on this PC** (8 shards side by side, each about as long as the others by the checks' measured times, `page/villageCosts.ts`), and about 5 on the lent 4080 laptop, which runs each check some 2.5 times slower. Dealt as equal counts of checks, its shards took 86 to 612 s and it ran 12 min 45 s there, the PC at a tenth of its CPU for most of it; in one browser it was 38 minutes of work (from the owner: "15min max, single threaded... then we shard, hopefully done in 2-3min"). `--shards n` deals another count, `--share k/n` takes this PC's share of the jobs for one check run on two PCs at once (`--share 1/2` here, `--share 2/2` through Loaner; `1,2/3` two shares of three), and `--jobs` more sessions than 8 where a PC boots them: this one doesn't (11 at once timed their boots out). **A check that draws a frame for each thing it reads is the first place to look when one is slow**: the hero's shadow by each lamp drew and read back 120 frames a stand, one for each patch of ground, and took 309 s of the suite's 2259 on the lent PC; read from one frame a state it takes 13 s here, its readings the same. **Thousands of rays go at chunks** (`page/rayChunks.ts`: a merged mesh cut by a grid, each chunk with bounds of its own, so three's own test skips what a ray passes by): sent at the merged meshes whole, the rays under the cottages' roofs were 73 s of a check's 78 (5 s now) and the hall's 27 thousand 55 s (28). **A walk that reads only where he goes runs `unposed`** (`page/unposed.ts`: his rig's animation skipped, 45% of such a check's time), never a check that reads or draws his body; eight do, every line of theirs as before. **A measure of thousands of frames keeps their bytes** (`drawRaw` in `page/light.ts`): made floats frame by frame, they were a quarter of `hallEntrySteady`. The suite is 779 s of browsers' work on this PC now, 13 minutes, from 38 on the lent one; find the next with `village --only <checks> --times --profile`. A smaller canvas (`--screen 960x540@1`) helped one check alone and moved its readings. Some checks' numbers follow the check before them (the arrows in the fountain, how much of him a wall hides): a new dealing of the shards moves them a little, each still within its limit.
- **Send a long run's output to a file** in `.tmp` (an `--ab` run, a check) and read its parts: read through `head` or `tail`, a run's end was lost.
- **Stop the server with `npm run lab -- stop`**: its supervisor starts a killed server again. It stops every command running in a process of its own from this tree too (`check`, `pwa`, one run without the server: `node/running.mjs`), which a stopped task doesn't: under Git Bash, `npm run lab` leaves its command no line back to the task, and a stopped check ran on to its end, eight browsers at full load.
- **Don't load the shared PC for more than about two minutes unasked** (the root's Workflow): a whole check of the old harness for a "before" number pinned the CPU and GPU for over 17 minutes. Take old timings from past runs' `out/check/durations.json` and logs, or time one narrow job.
- **The lab runs below normal priority, and lowers itself** (`node/priority.mjs`, #283): its command line, its server's supervisor and the server lower themselves as they start, before any browser, Vite or worker, which inherit it on Windows; each browser's processes Chrome raises (its GPU process as it starts, a page's renderer as it shows it) are lowered again after its launch and each page's load (`lowerProcesses` in `node/browser.mjs`, by the browser's own `SystemInfo.getProcessInfo`). At normal priority its runs froze the owner's desktop for 10 to 20 s, about a dozen times over one task. `lab status` and the supervisor's first line say what it runs at; check its browsers with their process tree's `PriorityClass` (a page's renderer may sit at Idle, lower still).

## Commands at a glance

- `cloud --google`: real sign-in controls/window and lightweight callback, with local OAuth replies: no game reload, one nonce-bound finish, link, cancel and blocked-popup redirect; `--canary` supplies an invalid code.

- `bank`: a bank on Random in the panels' lobby (#558): the wave in the save under the battleground rolled, offered as the starting wave (and read back from the save), Random rolling only where it is unlocked (`--canary` takes the bank's record out of the save).

- `dev`: developer controls, Hero/Dev tabs, five viewport layouts, frozen training start, refills and reset, and slow first-person entry (`--canary` plants a drained resource).

- `npm run lab:serve`: boots each command's page ahead of it (every command runs on a fresh boot and gives the same result every time)
- `npm run lab -- sheet`: pictures the hero in the player's views with each arm's load beside them
- `carry`: measures the carried bow
- `feet`: the feet against the ground round the dais's edges and on level ground (standing at full draw, walking across, strafing drawn, running reversals: out of reach, wobbling, sliding, in a riser, the rhythm)
- `range`: every joint against a body's ranges frame by frame, standing, walking, attacking and turning: each angle past its range and where, the frame's time, the weapon through his body, the arms at their stops (in full in `commands/range.md`)
- `eyes <skill>`: a skill cast through the eyes, frame by frame (where the blade goes across the view, wound up, through the blow and where it ends, how fast, where it is as the skill fires; a two-hander let go of by the other hand, seen or not, or seen in one hand; a fist open on its handle; the arm drawn against its swing's keys, the weapon's way and flat too; the arms' joints past their ranges where they're seen; the arms in the body the view hides, said; the weapon and the elbows jumping between frames; a film of the frames)
- `swing <name>`: a swing through the eyes keyed from a pose (the key that holds a grip and a weapon's way, and what of it the swing's plane can't hold; each key placed as the game places it at its share: the grip, the weapon and its share in the frame, the elbow, the wrist, a two-hander's lower grip against the other arm's reach, where it starts against the swing before's end; turned as the trunk turns the cut or its way back; the whole swing in 1% steps, an elbow turned over and a lower grip past reach between the keys; `all`: every swing of every weapon each way held, as `check` runs it)
- `match <picture>`: the hero standing through the eyes beside a reference's screenshot, on a screen of its shape (the screenshot, his frame, the two blended, the points marked on it and his drawn on each, and how far his are off)
- `coop`: two games playing together, a host's and a guest's, over a link of a high ping and jitter: what each shows of the other's heroes and foes and whether the hits counted, parties of 3 and 4, colours, trade, chat, pings, the run's end and spectating (`--scenarios`; in full in `commands/coop.md`)
- `guide`: the guide's first opening by G and a walk through every page (each frame and the page's long tasks by the browser's clock, at most 100 ms; the game's renderer links nothing), with none made before and the lobby's own building held: each page's own picture drawn within 150 ms of its opening or of its compile's end, every one within 20 s of G, and a spinner in every place still waiting (`--canary=stall` makes every picture again at once, `--canary=slow` makes them behind the lobby alone)

- `gait`: the walk judged as a person's, on level ground and the steps, drawn and not, tapping, turning and circling: each foot's time on the ground, the steps' rate, length and width, hops and gallops, the hips, the hands' swing, the jerks (in full in `commands/gait.md`)

- `evade`: the shooters getting out of the hero's attacks (each foe that evades held before him and each attack of his it evades cast at it try after try: from in front about half evaded, none struck after evading, every other struck; from behind none evaded; a ducked foe struck at its feet; each blink within its reach, clear of the props, the wall and the hero)
- `throws`: what the slammers throw at a hero too far off to slam (the Brute's lava at him stood still: landing where he stood as it was thrown, its ring there every frame it flies, its splash and pool hurting him; stepped off, nothing hurting him; up close, a slam and no throw; the Hulk's vine holding him for its hold, his walking going nowhere; stepped aside, never caught; stepped back onto it as it lies, held; a dash tearing him free; an arrow shot into it cutting it, `--class ranger`)
- `crowd`: a crowd of foes killed at once and what the frame costs as they fall (each frame timed by the browser's clock with the game drawn, the bodies' part apart, at each CPU slowdown, as a low-end device's, and frame rate)
- `draws`: one frame's draw calls with the crowd standing, by what drew them (the geometries drawn most often, those that drew the most triangles, the triangles drawn outside the view of the pass that drew them, the draws that drew nothing, the scene's draws by their material's look, through the eyes what the arms' own camera drew, the hero's arms and what they hold alone; the scene's objects whose matrices are made again each frame)
- `trace [code]`: a performance trace a slow game sent (`src/perf`), fetched from the traces service with the token in `tools/lab/traces.env` and read as the perf overlay's report reads (why it was sent, the boot's steps, the frame rate, timings, device and settings, the histories a second at a time, the worst stretches with what happened before each, the worst and long frames, the stages, and the `perf` command that plays it again on its settings); `--latest`, `--build <sha>`, `--list`, `--file` (runs in its own process, no browser)
- `auto`: Auto quality's walk down from High on a slow device (fed a GPU-bound device's frame times: each step, the time until it holds 50 fps, at most 20 s; a fast device with long hitches never stepped), and its boots from a level it settled on (the level above tried: kept on a fast device, left at once on a slow one, within 3.2 s on a phone just under the target there, and put off for 1, 2, 4, then 8 boots after each miss)
- `cloud`: cloud saves end to end, two devices each with a passkey against a saves service of the lab's own (an account made and slot 2 chosen as the cloud save, a second device signed in by a code with every slot full, a hero changed on both while one was offline, different heroes changed on each, the second device opened again cold with a change waiting in the cloud and its play saved before its warm-up ends (#556): each device's slots and the service's save read back; the bar and dialogs pictured within the window at desktops', a tablet's and a phone's sizes, `out/cloud`; `--canary` drops an item from a download, and the cloud's change from the hero played after the reboot)
- `recorder`: what the always-on performance recorder costs a frame in a fight (its calls timed against 0.05 ms a frame), the trace a send would make of it, sent to a traces service of the lab's own and read back with its token
- `perf [<skills>]`: skills cast in turn with the biome's foes coming, every frame timed as the game's performance report times it (the frame, the update by stage, the render, the GPU by pass, the draws, triangles and the particles' fill), on a phone's screen, boot and CPU if asked, or as a slow device players reported (`--device`: its frame rate there, its GPU played by this one drawing its screen at more pixels); `--village` the lobby's village standing and walking, `--fly` the dev tool's free camera flown over it (round it high and low and across it, twice: where each leg's dearest frames were)
- `programs`: the programs the boot compiled, from the game's dev-only ledger (how many each loading step made, behind the lobby too, by the warm-ups' compile or by a draw; each kind with the fields of its key that set its programs apart; those never drawn; those made after the reveal); `--cost`: each compiled again alone, salted so no cache answers, and timed (by kind, by what uses them, each step's by what uses them, by its point lights, the longest, a plain lit material by part)
- `compiles`: whether the boot's warm-up compiled what a fight draws (a prop of every kind broken, the first wave fought and a Brute's and a Hulk's throws in each biome, and again by a hero built afresh: every program compiled meanwhile, and the draw that first used it; the point lights each pass is lit by, against the boot's; `--switch` picks another class on the hero selection screen first, each hero shown in the hall of heroes, whose room and skills are warmed with the heroes)
- `maps`: the procedural maps a first visit's workers make, each timed alone with a digest of its pixels (a map made faster must keep every one: `--ab`), its seams (a repeated map must tile) and its means; `--pictures` shows each as a surface takes it
- `tiles`: how big each repeated map's tile comes out on each surface of a biome, in metres along u and v, how stretched and its pixels a metre (a surface's UVs decide it: size a map's repeat by it); `--pictures` shows each surface as the player meets it
- `winding`: which way every model's, the library's scholar's and biome's faces point (each mesh split into its parts, each judged by its signed volume: a part wound inside out on a one-sided material shows its inside)
- `wall`: how near the hero walks up to the wall (at angles round the ring he walks straight out until it stops him; the gap between his body and the nearest surface drawn)
- `rim`: the hero walked round the wall pressed into it, each way (a frame he moved further than a run takes him, thrown by the wall; a stop he can't walk back in from, stuck)
- `beyond`: the ranger's arrows loosed over the wall at the ground and the trees beyond it, over the shoulder; each arrow left standing must have a surface drawn at its point
- `trees`: the trees in the play area as the ranger's arrows meet them (every tree inside the wall a wooden prop, an arrow shot at each one's trunk standing in it; then another biome shown, and no arrow left)
- `columns`: the crypt's broken columns roll after roll (no open edge in a mesh, no cliff across a break's face)
- `shadows`: the hero's shadow cast as one against his pieces cast alone (each weapon style, armoured and bare, each view: the frame drawn both ways, pixels apart past a texel's rounding; `--cost` the shadow map's draw)
- `gear`: full sets of gear rolled for every class at each tier (the Health, regeneration and draught they give, and the damage they multiply)
- `weapons`: each of the hero's weapons alone in a lit studio (face on, edge on, from behind, from three-quarters and its head close up, with its size and its triangles)
- `hands`: the hero's right hand alone in the studio, flat, open, relaxed, in a fist and round a handle, from each side and close up (its size against a man's, its triangles, where it was sculpted)
- `sound [scenes]`: a skill's sounds rendered offline as the game plays them and written as WAV files to listen to (Splintering Bolt held and its bursts, Void Lance's portal, beam and release, or any sound of the game's played once: each one's peak, loudest half second, level over time, brightness (centroid, share above 2 kHz), share above 150 Hz, most tonal peak, the nodes it made, its silence at its end)
- `music`: each biome's music as the player hears it (its lobby, a run's countdowns, fights and wins, a hidden tab and the bank back: which track plays, how it fades, where it comes in, what reaches WebAudio)

Options every command takes:

- `--screen WxH@ratio`: puts a command's page on a screen of that size and pixel ratio
- `--insets l,t,r,b`: with `--screen`, the screen's safe-area insets (css px), as a phone's camera cutout gives them
- `--renderers webgl,webgpu`: runs it on this tree booted on two renderers, one after the other, and compares them as `--ab` compares two commits (`parity`)
- `--screens a,b`: runs it on two screens and compares them as `--ab` compares two commits (`ui --screens 3840x2160@1,1280x720@3`: a 4K screen at 100% and 300%)
- `--ab`: runs any of them on `origin/main` too and says which lines and pictures differ (a commit against itself agrees, line for line), and for each picture that differs, where and how bright this side is there against main's (`×0.72 as bright there`: a look kept or lost, `node/pictures.mjs`)
- `--watch`: runs one again on each change to the source
- `--profile`: says where a command's time went, and keeps the profile whole in `out/profile/<command>-<side>.cpuprofile`, every sample with its time (DevTools' Performance panel loads it): the summary adds a run up, and half a second of slow frames is lost in it
- `--tune name.field=v,...;...`: sets the game's tunable values before a command's set-up (`src/core/tuning.ts`: `warriorEyes.oneOn=0.75,-1.1,0.5`), so a pose or a camera is compared across values in seconds, without editing the source and booting again; `name.field[i,j]=v` sets some of a list's numbers alone (a swing's keys); given twice, the two are joined (any other option given twice is refused: a second `--tune` once replaced the first unsaid)
- `--sweep name.field=a|b|c;...`: runs a command once for each value, or each combination of several, over its `--tune`, on the lab's server, each run's problems in full and the runs with the fewest named (a filter on the runs' output hid three failures on the runs it called best)
- `--boot level[@pixels]`: the page booted as a device that settled on that quality (`low@518400`: #243's phone, its pixels let go): the level set as the player's setting before the boot, so its warm-ups run at that level and the hero is built at its detail (not as Auto's cookie: a boot of Auto's tries the level above first, and the lab's steady frames would keep it); a page booted ahead counts as ready only for the same boot
- `--first`: the page booted as a player's first visit: no saved hero, so the game boots its default one (`--class` it: `--class mage`) and opens on the hero selection screen, cold as `--cold` leaves it. The lab's own boot saves a hero (the class cookie), so it never met the first visit's boot: `programs --first` named the other heroes' programs a first visit's hall made at a draw, a task of 2.7 s, which no other boot showed
- `--renderer webgl|webgpu|meshlets`: the page booted on that renderer, at the address the pause menu's switch opens (`?renderer=webgpu`, `&meshlets=1` too); a page booted ahead counts as ready only for the same renderer
- `--panels`: the page booted with the lobby as it is without the village beta (`src/core/beta.ts`): its panels over the battleground. The lab boots the village by default (the beta's cookie, `openPageIn` in `node/side.mjs`), which its lobby's commands measure (`village`, `ui`, `practice`, `coop`...); a page booted ahead counts as ready only for the same lobby. `light --panels --ab` holds the lobby without it to main's frames: the same draws of random numbers and the same light, its pictures apart where a run's seed and the hero's idle differ
- `--cold`: the page booted as a player's first visit (or a new build's) leaves it: the lab's own boot asks for everything the game warms after the lobby shows (`src/game/warm.ts`: the fight's programs, the other heroes') and waits for it; a cold one asks for nothing, so those stay cold until something needs them, as in the owner's lobby (`programs --cold`: its performance report counted every program as compiled late)
- `--rarity rare|...` (any command that sets the run up): every item he wears of that rarity, as its look shows it (Rare and up glow)
- `--wear helm,chest,hands|none` (any command that sets the run up): the armour the hero wears, every piece not named left off (the set-up's report says what he `wears`): with `--screen` at the owner's window, a picture of what they see (the lab's hero is otherwise as the boot rolled him, bare-handed, and his first-person hands looked nothing like the owner's gloved ones)
- `houses`: the village's houses walked into and out of by E held at their doors in each view (a tap taking him nowhere, the prompt naming the house and filling; no two faces round a door inside in one plane; the room shown, the village and the yard's dummies not, every frame drawn and no program compiled, its cut away walls, a walk held in by its back wall, the trophies for the bosses beaten alone, the ledger's card within the window, the party table opening Play together, the library's Codex opening the guide, its scholar talked to and his card within the window, out before the cottage's door, the yard's dummies still on their spots, and the square and yard pictured after); `--canary` compiles a program going in, keeps the dummies in the room's walls, leaves the room drawn going out, makes the hold a press, lays a sheet in the door frame, makes the talk card wider than the window and lets the room's zoom go (from above in a room, the wheel out and in kept to its range, his own zoom given back out of it). `coop --scenarios houses`: the guest goes into the tavern, the host's game shows his name over its door, a dot at it on the minimap and his name under it on the map, all gone as he comes out (`node/coopHouses.mjs`, `page/coopHouses.ts`; `--canary` takes the marker away).
- `scholar`: the library's Brother Hollis (his reading loop posed through frame by frame, its wrap included: no joint snapping, his head and hands moving, his sleeves never through his table, the book's prints reaching the spine, the page he turns never under the book and landing flat, his fingers on its edge while he holds it, the page never through either hand, his forearms clothed to the wrist, his fingertips never sunk into what they rest on; a hero come near glanced up at and walked off from, the hero held in his eye while talked to; pictured at the loop's beats close up from each side and over his shoulder, his face, and as the player sees him); `--film` a picture a moment; `--canary` jumps his loop on mid-way, raises his table, lowers the page's spine, draws the prints back from the spine, drops the page on his resting hand and strips his undersleeves.
- `merchant`: Odo's prices against the drop rates (no rarity sold from a wave where trading beats playing for it, none above epic) and his trade at his counter (the screen within the window, its three steps lit in turn and the stash dimmed till a ware is chosen, the legendary not offered, Auto-fill cheapest paying with rares, his full stock, a trade one common short held off and refused, one with enough made and saved, the stock one fewer, his restock after three runs; Odo pictured close and over the shoulder); `--canary` puts the piece bought back in his stock and leaves the stash dimmed with a ware chosen.
- `training`: Training grounds (#405): attempted-wave persistence/migration, the village's ghost talked to and its card's battleground and wave controls, harmless projections, no rewards or records, repeat and leave; `--canary` plants ordinary damage and a solid ghost.
- `guard`: #399, attacks while blocking in third person and through the eyes, a damage control during the exposed swing, immediate return/release/failure cases, independent shield and attack touch fingers. `--canary` leaves a shield raised during a swing and must detect it.
- `portraits`: optional portrait requests and the real co-op picker; no downloads before use, current class only, selection, peer picture rendering and clearing, a class's default picture shown with none chosen, no two classes' alike (run it with `--panels`: the village's lobby hides the party cards it pictures). `--canary` requests another class and must detect it.

A command exits with 1 when it failed or found a problem (an error in the page, a canary missed; not the dev server's own client's, whose socket the lab never uses: on a machine short of sockets it failed to connect, and failed jobs the game had passed, `fromDevClient` in `node/side.mjs`). An A/B's pictures that differ are pictured where they differ (`tools/lab/out/ab/<command>/`), a probe can return moments pictured (`lab.picture`, or `lab.screen` for the screen as the player sees it, the page's UI over the game, whole or round one element; written as a sheet and compared in an A/B), and `page/timing.ts` times a method's calls by the browser's own clock (the page's `performance.now` is the lab's). Write a new measure in the lab (`tools/lab/examples/handSpeed.ts` shows its parts: the state it measures checked to have happened, a canary it must catch), never as a script with a boot and a clock of its own, and keep it: from its first use it is a lab command, or part of one that measures the same thing, never a probe left in `.tmp` (every harness is permanent). The probes and films the AGENTS.md files and their notes name (the bow probe, the range probe, `legdet`, the zigzag and aim probes...) were such scripts and aren't in the repo: each is a measure to port into the lab the first time it's needed again, as the carry's was (`carry`). The lab's own code has unit tests: `npm run lab:test`.
