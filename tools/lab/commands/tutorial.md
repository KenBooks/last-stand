# `tutorial`

One command of the lab in full, moved here from [`COMMANDS.md`](../COMMANDS.md) (which lists every command, and the
options every command takes) for its size.

The village's tutorial (`src/game/tutorial.ts`) walked as a player walks it (`page/tutorial.ts`), as
`--class`'s hero, with the keys and the mouse or (`--touch`, with a phone's `--screen`) by a finger: the notice
board used and its tutorial begun, then each step done the way its own words say: the bag opened (I, the Bag
button), a worn item thrown on the ground (dragged, as the page hears a drag: its start on the item and its drop
on the game's field; on touch its sheet's Drop), its name clicked (tapped twice; stepped round first where the
touch controls lie over it), the stash opened at the chest, an item of its kind put on; the yard walked into, a
dummy struck (a bow's shot drawn and let go), three skills used, the hero's own defence against the sparring
shade (a warrior's shield held up to a blow, then to one that breaks his guard: he must be staggered; a mage's
daze or freeze; a ranger's lure), the draught; a skill put on a key in the bag (a short screen's rail turned to
its skills first), the hall of heroes used and left by its Back (waited for in real time), the chronicle's and a
portal's cards opened, a run of one wave entered by the portal's card and banked by the choice's button, and the
campfire's Play together opened (on the lab's link, `?net=local`) and closed. Before each step is done the tracker is read (the step it asks is
the one expected, its words filled in, within the window and over none of the minimap, the touch buttons and the
bottom bar) and pictured (`out/tutorial/sheet.png`). A step not ticked by doing it, health lost to the shade, a
shade that outstays the walk, a program compiled on the way, progress the cookie doesn't keep and a board that
doesn't say each station is done are problems. From above (`setView('top')`), where the mouse aims. `--canary`
plants a tracker as tall as two windows and an emptied cookie, each of which must be found.
