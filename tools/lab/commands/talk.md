# `talk`

One command of the lab in full, kept here rather than in [`COMMANDS.md`](../COMMANDS.md) (which lists every
command, and the options every command takes) for that file's size.

A whole conversation with the library's Brother Hollis as a player has it, and then each of the folk's portraits (`page/folkFaces.ts`: Brother Hollis's, and Odo's once his shop is built): each built and staged as his card's picture is, his head facing the camera within 6 degrees and each eye on it within 8 ("Have him look straight"; `--canary` turns each head 30 degrees off the camera after staging, which must be found). The conversation itself, as a player has it (`page/talk.ts`; `src/ui/talk.ts`, what he says in `src/data/talk.ts`). In by the library's door (E held), a hero who's never talked to him nor fought: his portrait made behind the lobby first (the frames on with a little of the browser's own time each: its compile and encoding run on it), then E at his table opens a first meeting (`meet`), his portrait's picture shown in the oval, his lines each followed frame by frame as they show, one a frame at most and none before its reading time (`readTime`, judged by the game's own times taken before any canary), as many nods as lines (`scholarTalk.lines`), his face within 25 degrees of the hero, the replies offered only once he's done, "What do you make of me?" not among them. Then asked through: 1 (himself), Enter while he speaks showing the rest, the arrow down lighting "Why stay here?" and Enter picking it, a click on his lines showing the rest, "Something else..." and the rumour clicked (a door behind the great hall), its follow-up by its key, Leave (the last reply) clicked: the talk closed and, 4 s on, his face back at his book. The profile read back from storage keeps one visit and every node heard. A visit after, the hero given three runs: greeted as a fellow conspirator (he'd heard of the door), the rumour marked asked and, tapped, answered otherwise, and "What do you make of me?" by its key naming his best wave. Every state's card and replies within the window, each reply the element at its middle; Esc ends it without the pause menu. The game's renderer compiles no program over the whole talk. The frame's cost with the card up against without it (a game frame and a browser frame each, the browser's clock). It pictures the first meeting, a close look at him looking up and reading again after, the choice lit, the rumour, the visit after and his take on the hero. `--canary` shows every line at once and pushes Leave below the window, each of which must be found.
