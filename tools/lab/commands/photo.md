The rest of the commands are in [`COMMANDS.md`](../COMMANDS.md).

Photo mode as a player uses it (`page/photo.ts`, `src/ui/photo.ts`), in the village at dusk, then from the village's pause menu, the Thornwood and the crypt, each over the shoulder. In each place: opened by P (on, the solo game paused, the grade's look on); every element of the page outside photo mode's panel and the game's canvas that a player would see (a box, visible, opaque, with text or a background of its own) listed, none allowed (the pad covers the screen, so what's under it can't be found by a point); the world's overlays drawn in no frame (`overFrame.draw` counted with the frames drawn); the game's clock and the hero still over 30 frames; the camera flown at least 1 m by W in 30 frames and turned 1 rad by a 200 px drag on its pad (within 0.05); the contrast slider and the Cinematic filter setting the grade's uniforms, drawn, with no program compiled; the time of day settable in the village and greyed out in a battleground; Hide hero putting every mesh of his on the hidden layer and back; a photo by Space caught as the page hands it to the browser (the download itself left out): named `last-stand-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMSS.png`, at the canvas's pixels, its middle lit, and once letterboxed its bar's row black. Then a shot is set up (the letterbox, a filter, the vignette, the hour in the village, the panel hidden) and pictured, a photo taken of it, and Esc must turn it off, the grade's look and vignette the game's, the page's UI back and the game going on; opened from the pause menu's button, Esc must bring the pause menu back. P with How to play open must open nothing. In each of the village's houses (`enterRoom`, as its door takes him in): opened by P, the time of day greyed out, what the camera turns round sent 40 m out by a frame of W and kept inside the walls, the camera drawn 40 m back and kept at most 6 m beyond them, a wall cut away with it out there; pictured so, a dollhouse. By touch alone (in the village, after its first tap puts the page in touch mode): the pause button, its Settings tab and Photo mode tapped open it, the touch keys' line shown (but on a short screen, which leaves it out), a finger's 200 px drag on the pad turns it 1 rad as the mouse's does, two fingers spread from 40 to 160 px bring it to at most half its distance, and Leave tapped brings the pause menu back. The village is pictured with the camera down at the ground and 60 m back too (`village-low`, `village-far`). Pictured as it opens and with the shot set up, in each place (`out/photo`). `--canary` takes `body.photo` off and draws the overlays again, each to be caught.
